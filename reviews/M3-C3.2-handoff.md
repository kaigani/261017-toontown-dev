## 2. Behaviour notes a reviewer will want

- **Frames.** Every command frame is a scene frame in the first cycle (05 §19.1.4); keys are stored local. Results report
  scene frames; `NodeEval.localFrame` is the local frame before the cycle mapping (SI-098). A cell-based element's local
  frame is its scene frame, over the scene length.
- **Exactness.** A keyframe's frame returns its stored point or value; zero handles and the `linear` preset are computed as
  lines; multiples of 90° are exact. So E4's exact poses agree to the last bits with `fractions`.
- **Velocity.** Stored normalised to the keyframe segment; reported in fields. A key record at a pin frame carries shape only.
  Inserting, removing, unlocking or moving a pin renormalises with the old arc lengths (05 §10.5). When `key.move` moves a
  pin, intermediate keys move proportionally within their segments, rounded half up, later wins.
- **Passage frames.** Whole frames are scanned forward first, then bisected to 1e-4, so an earlier crossing of a
  non-monotonic curve is found, at whole-frame resolution. Constant segments report their start frame (SI-099).
- **`world` positions** target the node's position `P`, not its pivot-displaced origin (`anim.transform.set`).
- **Cut-out rigs articulate through pegs:** 04 §9.2 and C3.1's SI-092 refuse children under drawings, and a reviewer test
  pins that, so E4's drawings are leaves. A document whose tree already nests under drawings evaluates through them.
- **Refusals found by the suites.** E4 found a debug-only panic: `then_some` evaluated `i − 1` eagerly on a one-key path
  queried after its key (fixed, with a maths test). Without E4 the in-process suites had not reached that branch.
- **Timings.** `c32_budgets` records single observations on shared runners (`evidence/M3-C3.2/`), not bounds. The criterion
  is an answer within the 30-second watchdog: a result or a typed refusal, with no revision change.
