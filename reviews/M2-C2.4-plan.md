## 0. Questions for the orchestrator (work proceeds on the stated default until ruled)

1. **`draw.vectorFile.import` owner.** The registry declares it with owner "M2 C2.4". Plan §4 places vector import
   (PDF/AI/SVG) in **M10 C10.2**, and the C2.4 row and readiness list do not mention it. Default: re-own it to
   M10 C10.2 (declaration, traceability, handoff), since a PDF/AI parser is M10 scope.
2. **The 100k flatten gate (C22-T2).** The plan has no millisecond budget for a 100k-stroke edit; §4 and M11 C11.1 say
   "performance targets … 100 k strokes". Default gate: a 100k Flatten **completes** when its release wall time on the
   CI runner is at most the p50 of `strokeAdd` in the same run's 100k series (today 18.9 s on Windows) **and** below the
   30 s watchdog with the work budget unchanged in kind. Otherwise refusal stays atomic and before the watchdog, and the
   gap is reported with raw numbers.
3. **Vectorize undo granularity.** 02 §11.2 says cancelling keeps completed drawings, so each image commits on its own.
   Default: one undo step per image, labelled "Import and Vectorize", committed through the executor from the job.
4. **Feather path.** SI-077 Design B needs a 10 §4.9 amendment (a per-object opacity), so under the C2.4 ruling
   `draw.feather.apply` stays declared with owner "M2 C2.4 — blocked on SI-077" unless the spec owner answers first.

## 2. Topology property tests (item 2)

Permanent suite `crates/vector/tests/c24_properties.rs` (plus `crates/executor/tests/c24_topology.rs` for operations
that only exist as commands), proptest `TestRunner` with fixed ChaCha seeds and shrinking, as in `c22_properties.rs`.

- **Generators:** segment/polyline arrangements on a small integer grid that force degeneracies: shared endpoints,
  T-junctions, collinear overlapping edges, coincident duplicate strokes, shared edges between closed shapes, near
  misses within and outside `JUNCTION_TOLERANCE`; brush outlines with holes; generated bitmaps for vectorize.
- **Independent oracle:** an exact rational arrangement built separately from the product (segments split at all exact
  intersections with `num-rational`/i128, DCEL faces by angular order, signed areas): zone count, exact areas,
  boundary stroke/side sets, and the canonical zone ID recomputed from the boundary (the `topology_oracle` hashing
  reimplemented in the test crate). Extends the C2.2 oracles rather than replacing them.
- **Properties per operation** (vectorize, stroke add/set/delete, cut ×3 primitives, eraser, flatten, optimize, smooth,
  lines to brush, centre line, break apart, transform with determinant +1 and −1 (reflection), envelope, point-subset
  transforms with junction classes, snap to contour): oracle equality; idempotent settle; stroke-order independence;
  zone ID canonicity under translation by whole grid units; exact-overlap fill carry-over with the lower-ID tie rule;
  SI-059 kept whole; `zone_at(interior)`; incremental map == full map (§4).
- Every failing seed found during development is recorded in the handoff with its shrunk case and kept as a fixed
  regression test.

## 3. E2 headless chain (item 3)

`scripts/e2-paint-by-id.py <toontown-headless> <out-dir>` with the official SDK (`mcp==2.0.0`) against the **release**
binary: create a project and a drawing element; `draw.vectorize.run` on `tests/fixtures/vectorize/e2-lineart.png`
(job, progress, `job_wait`); `doc.validate {checks:["unpaintedZones"]}` lists the zones; paint each zone by ID;
validation clean; `colour.recolour.apply`; `render_drawing` (generic tool) twice with identical hashes and known pixels
at fixed coordinates; a stale zone ID and a stale revision are refused; each mutation is one revision and one event;
undo/redo; save, reopen, equality of `doc.export.json`; export the batch log and replay it with `toontown-headless --run`
from the identical saved base, byte-identical result. Seven PNGs (vectorize preview, after vectorize, after paint, after
recolour, after undo, after redo, after reopen) and their SHA-256 hashes go to `out/e2/`.

## 4. C22-T2 incremental re-noding (item 4)

Today every changed layer re-nodes, rebuilds the graph and re-carries fills for **all** its strokes. Plan:

1. **Noding:** pair tests only where at least one edge belongs to a changed stroke (persistent noding already leaves
   unchanged strokes split at their mutual junctions), using the spatial grid of the changed strokes' boxes against a
   cached per-layer edge grid.
2. **Graph:** connected components of the arrangement whose edges are untouched keep their half-edges, faces, zones and
   IDs from the cached map; only the components touched by changed or removed edges are rebuilt, then merged with
   face/hole nesting recomputed for the affected region.
3. **Fill carry-over:** limited to zones of rebuilt components.
4. **Flatten:** merges layer maps instead of re-settling the merged stroke list three times.
5. **Equivalence proof:** a property test compares incremental and full maps (pieces, zone IDs, fills, sides) under
   seeded edit sequences, and a debug assertion mode runs both on every settle in the test suite.

Raw 100k and planar worst-case observations are kept on both OSes. If the gate of question 2 is not reached, the gap is
reported with numbers; refusal stays atomic and before the watchdog.

## 5. C23-T1 medial axis (item 5)

Constrained Delaunay triangulation of each spineless brush outline (holes included) with exact orientation/incircle
predicates on the snapped grid (own implementation in `ops/centre.rs`, deterministic across OSes; the `spade` crate
is the fallback if the own CDT does not meet the corpus); medial axis from the triangles (junction/sleeve/terminal
classification, chordal axis); prune branches shorter than the local width (decision 12); split where the source colour
changes; pencil lines with the source colour and the average width (area ÷ axis length). Spine-based output and the
fill-only warning are kept; `centreLineNoSpine` disappears only for outlines the CDT supports, with the refusal reason
kept for degenerate ones. Undo, save/open and topology/fill carry-over tests.

## 6. C21-R1-11 symmetry corpus (item 7)

`symmetry_setting` reads the global symmetry settings (02 decision 5) through the configured commands, so
`symmetry: true` produces real mirror and kaleidoscope maps. A corpus of command-created strokes for several axis
angles and 2–16 mirrors, digested on Windows and macOS (golden digests compared across OSes in CI), plus same-font
text-layout controls; both seeded 10k topology histories retained.

## 7. R1-12 budgets and item 9 (items 8, 9)

`c24_measure.rs` (release, raw JSON under `target/evidence/c24/`): edit/undo/redo/dry-run at 100k, retained memory and
OS RSS (Windows `GetProcessMemoryInfo`, macOS `task_info` through the existing platform crates, test-only), queued-read
delay behind a running edit, maintenance/autosave delay, Smooth and conversions, cancellation in queue/running/batch
states, typed MCP timeout and audit delivery, replay exclusion of timed-out atomic batches, the 100k-character Break
Apart and the size-limit render with caller-refusal and following-read timings separately. Budgets are stated as
measured bounds with headroom, not invented targets; glyph preparation, result staging and cleanup are precharged or
bounded where the measurements show a long tail. M11 C11.1 keeps the final scale gate.

## 8. Work budgets

`VECTORIZE_WORK_BUDGET` (per image, decode excluded, decode bounded by its own limits), sized from the corpus so a
16 384² worst case refuses before the watchdog; trace charges per pixel visited and per contour vertex; fitting charges
through `fit_bounded`; settle through `PLANAR_WORK_BUDGET`. Medial axis: per outline, charged per triangle and per axis
vertex. Incremental noding charges what it actually does, so its refusals stay before the watchdog.
