## 7. Residual risks

- A Select drag counts as "on the selection" inside the selection's bounding box, not its outlines.
- Headless, one call's Polyline clicks are one gesture; the GUI keeps a Polyline open across input (C4.2b).
- The static light table lives in session state until M5 C5.2.
- Holds are measured as round trips from the calling thread on an idle executor; the render-list holds are C4.2b's.
- Known timing flakes as in the C4.1 handoff (rerun once, always report).
