## 3. What was built

- **`camera_view.rs` (new).** The Camera view chain (view pixels, camera space, camera output pixels, a drawing's
  units, scene points through the camera), `chain_at` for an addressed frame, and the refusals. `addressing` and
  `addressing_for` are the one entry the addressing parameters, the colour commands' frames, `view.*`, `inspect.*`,
  `draw.viewMapping.get` and availability use.
- **`addressing.rs`.** `target`: the element and frame a call addresses (SI-146). `check`: the common addressing check
  that availability evaluates.
- **The scene answer (`canvas.rs`).** Form 1.1 (`docs/canvas-format.md` §11).
- **Commands.** `anim.tool.set`, `anim.tool.get`, `anim.tool.changed` (event) and `anim.view.coords.get`. Also
  `anim.transform.set`'s labels and `draw.viewMapping.get` for Camera views.
- **Gestures.** `scene_gesture.rs` holds the scene tools, the temporary Skew and the vertical shear. `gesture.rs` runs
  Camera views, picks the family, and refuses a drawing tool on an unprojectable plane.
- **Renders and captures.** `activeView` with a Camera view; the headless capture.
- **Front end.** `desktop/src/canvas/scene.ts` decodes form 1.1 with R1-R7.
- **Performance.** Three costs that were quadratic in the scene's elements are linear now (evaluator scene length,
  hidden-ancestor search, pick prefilter by `extent`), and a scene gesture builds one render list instead of two.

## 9. The D-24 note: where the occurrence table lives

Ruling D-24 names "TTRD 1.1 (the occurrence table …)". The occurrences are per-request state of a view: which drawing,
through which matrix, in which order. The scene answer (`canvas_scene`, JSON) already carries the Drawing view's
entries, while TTRD is the drawing payload (`canvas_load`, binary), resident per drawing and unchanged by a camera move.
So the occurrence table is in the scene answer, versioned as **form 1.1**, with:

- its own version rule and members;
- validation rules R1-R7;
- what a 1.0 reader does;
- a golden corpus with a corrupt file per rule and per ranged member.

All of this is in `docs/canvas-format.md` §11, normative before the encoder. **TTRD stays 1.0** and its corpus is
unchanged. The homography tolerance and the near plane are stated in the camera-view note §2 and in SI-145: no
homography exists, since the view uses the renderer's affine plane mapping. This departs from the letter of D-24, not
from its intent. If the orchestrator wants the table inside TTRD, it is a C4.3b change.
