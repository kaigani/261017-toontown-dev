### C32-R1-01 — P1 — Passage inversion misses an earlier subframe crossing

Clause: 05 §10.5 requires the smallest frame with V(f) ≥ the control-point arc length, including non-monotonic velocity, to 1e-4 frame, rounded to three decimals.

Reproduction: `cargo test -p tt-anim --locked -j 4 --test c32_r1_passage`. New independent test `c32_r1_passage_must_find_subframe_first_crossing` intentionally stays failing. Raw result: `evidence/M3-C3.2-r1/passage.txt`.

A straight unit path has pins at frames 1 and 3. A spline velocity key at frame 2 has value zero. Frame-1 right and frame-2 left handles are `(1/3,1)` and `(-1/3,1)`. On [1,2], the independent Bernstein formula reduces to x(u)=1+u, V(u)=3u(1-u). For arc 1/2, the first crossing is `1+(1-sqrt(1/3))/2`, reported **1.211**. Observed: **2.500**. Integer endpoint samples miss the complete excursion between frames 1 and 2.

Fix direction: bracket all possible crossings within each velocity segment using its extrema and boundaries, then solve the earliest bracket. Keep charged work and explicit convergence/refusal budgets. Whole-frame scanning does not establish earliest crossing for a non-monotonic cubic. The handoff's whole-frame qualification is weaker than the normative smallest-frame requirement.
