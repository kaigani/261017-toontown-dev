**C11-R2-02 — P1 — Array append and nested array-move inverses are not invertible.** `crates/model/src/change.rs:509` constructs inverses without enough array-location context. Starting with `{"a":[1,2]}`, an accepted `add /a/- 3` produces inverse `remove /a/-`, which fails. Starting with `{"a":[{"x":1},{"x":2},{"x":3}]}`, accepted `move from /a/0 to /a/1/x`, `oldValue:3`, produces an inverse result `{"a":[{"x":1},{"x":3},{}]}` rather than the original. Re-insertion shifts the destination before restoration. This violates spec 10 §2.6's invertibility requirement and leaves R1-04 open. Record/resolve concrete append and shifted restoration locations when constructing reversible changes; the inverse must restore the exact input. **Regressions:** M `array_append_patch_inverse_restores_input` (line 267), `nested_array_move_with_overwrite_inverse_restores_input` (272). **Due:** C1.1 retry.
