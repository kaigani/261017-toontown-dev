### C24-R2-01 domains (`crates/executor/tests/c24_route_domains.rs`)

Each property generates its starting state through the commands, paints every zone, applies the command and checks the
read-back. **Exact** means the expected snap-rounded map is built from the persisted start state and the command:
- the product's zones must be its zone faces, with equal snapped areas;
- the product face at each interior point must be the same region, with equal area and corners;
- boundary strokes and canonical ID come from that output face, because where the product cuts its pieces along an
  expected edge is not predicted;
- fills follow the carry-over rule from the start state's map.

**Two-stage** means (a) the semantics predict the output geometry and the zones within the stated tolerance, then (b)
the exact snap-rounded map of the independently flattened output must equal the product's zones (partition, areas,
boundaries, IDs), with carried fills.

The invisible centre covers the loop exactly once. Zones: one per rectangle face, area within the band.
