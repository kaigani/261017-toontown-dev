## CI follow-up (run 41191607130 at `9d57fd9`: `check` failed on both OSes)

1. **Windows: `c21_r3_contracts::closed_step_pressure_command_returns_within_watchdog` exceeded 30 s.** This is the
   reviewer's closed decagon (radius 30) under a 100 du round tip with 99 000 alternating pressure knots, `fit: false`.
   Locally it took 18.5 s in debug and 14.5 s in release, all of it in the union. Every tip covers the centre, so the
   64 shapes in each overlay call all cross one another, and one call's work grows with its vertices times its
   crossing shapes: about 3 µs per vertex, against the 128 ns my per-vertex charge assumed. **Fix `c1adff9`:**
   - `UNION_CHUNK` 64 → 4 (measured: 8 → 2.9 s, 4 → 1.8 s, 2 → 1.4 s in release; 4 keeps fewer levels).
   - The overlay charge is now per vertex **and** shape of each call, still charged before the call.
   - Exactly collinear vertices that an intermediate union leaves on a boundary edge are removed.
   - The decagon is in the brush corpus.

   Result: the command takes **2.9 s debug / 2.0 s release** (worst accepted outline 1.77 s), and the budget is
   unchanged. Each intermediate call snaps its new intersection vertices to the 1/64 du grid. So the united boundary
   can differ from a single call's by about a grid step, which is why the golden changed (above). ADR-0016 §5 now says
   this instead of "the same point set", which round 2 claimed.
2. **macOS: `tt-persist` unit test `lock::tests::a_protected_lock_left_by_a_dead_holder_is_taken_over_and_its_heartbeat_fails_closed`
   saw the lock still held the instant after its handle closed.** On Unix a `flock` belongs to the open file
   description, and a process spawned at that moment by another thread shares that description until close-on-exec.
   Here the spawner is the parallel tests' `kill -0` liveness probes. So the lock frees only once that reference is
   gone. Round 3 does not touch `tt-persist`, and the test passed in the four previous CI runs. **`d57e746`** makes this
   builder-owned M1 unit test wait, bounded to 2 s, for the probe to report the lock free before asserting it. The
   asserted property is unchanged. Product behaviour is unchanged and acceptable: a takeover attempted in that instant
   reports the lock held, and the next attempt succeeds.
