**Limits of the exact oracle, stated:**
- Fitted brush outlines are curves.
- Lines to Brush and Centre Line are checked on a converted line outside every zone, where the oracle predicts the line
  layer exactly, and Centre Line must return the exact spine line.
- Break Apart is checked by the line layer staying exactly the oracle's, plus each glyph layer's zone count, membership
  and fill.
- Snap to Contour into a segment's interior lands off the grid. Its junction then depends on the snapped crossing, so
  those cases are skipped and snaps onto anchors are checked exactly.
- Arrangements that are not snap-safe (a vertex within 6 grid units of a non-incident edge, or two vertices snapping
  together) are skipped before the command runs.
