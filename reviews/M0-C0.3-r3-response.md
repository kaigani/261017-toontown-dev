## C03-R3-01 — valid one-sample completion rejected — **fixed as ruled**

**Analyzer** (`scripts/analyse-canvas-log.py`):
- **Validation:**
  - a version-2 run needs **at least one** pan/zoom sample. Zero samples is still rejected as malformed ("pan/zoom
    frames missing"), even when the run claims a complete span;
  - completion is judged on the span alone: it must last its declared duration, its samples must lie on it, and the
    last sample must be the span's end;
  - version-1 logs keep the two-sample minimum, because without a span the samples are the only proof of completion.
- **Cadence:**
  - estimating cadence needs two samples spanning time;
  - otherwise the run's analysis records `complete: true`, `cadence: "insufficient samples (n=1)"`, `fps: null`,
    `intervalMs: null` and `meetsTarget: null`;
  - the report prints that text in the fps column and "not estimated: insufficient samples (n=1) (run complete)" in
    the target column;
  - two equal timestamps read "insufficient samples (n=2, zero-length span)". This also removes a division by zero
    the old estimator would have hit;
  - no frames are made up, and nothing claims 60 fps or "missed";
  - runs with two or more samples use the unchanged estimator.

**Page** (`CanvasSpike.tsx`): `summarizeRun` already returned `fps = null` and `meetsTarget = null` for one sample. The
readout and the status line now say "insufficient samples (n=1)" / "complete; cadence not estimated" instead of a
dash. The logged data is unchanged.

**Tests:**
- reviewer: `C03-R3-01: completed 0.1 Hz phase …` and the 0.2 Hz control pass;
- the R2 page tests (manual, 60 Hz, 1 Hz "missed") still pass, as do R1 tooling 12/12 and R2 tooling 9/9;

  - **zero frames** under a complete span is rejected with "pan/zoom frames missing";
  - also rejected: one frame in a version-1 log, one frame with a span shorter than declared, and one frame that is
    not on the span's end.
