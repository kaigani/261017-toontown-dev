(d) Passage touch: 05 ?9 defines the sampled arc length, and ?10.5 requires the earliest frame reaching that arc to 1e-4 frame, reported to three decimals. That is a time tolerance. The new independent quadratic family has exact maximum 3/4 at frame 3/2 and then a linear rise from zero at frame 2 to one at frame 3. The oracle is analytic: for arc <=3/4, first crossing is `1+(1-sqrt(1-4*arc/3))/2`; above 3/4, first crossing is `2+arc`. Candidate scan tolerance and frame accuracy are adjudicated separately: exact maxima at/above the arc must retain the earliest crossing; maxima below by more than PASSAGE_TOUCH must report the later crossing. Within the documented touch band the closure permits the candidate touch, even though the exact mathematical crossing is later; the 1e-4 frame tolerance alone would not justify that approximation.
