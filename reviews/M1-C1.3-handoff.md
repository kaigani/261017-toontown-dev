## What was built (summary; details in ADR-0014)

- **`tt-persist`** (new crate code):
  - a hostile-input-safe zip reader and writer (ZIP64, `mimetype` first, safe names, bounded decompression, no
    encryption);
  - the manifest and versions, and both container forms;
  - the load sequence with deterministic JSON-level repairs and the load report;
  - the package writer (temporary file, verify, staged backup, rename) and the journaled folder writer;
  - backups, recovery copies, locks, per-user settings, libraries, templates and palette files;
  - migrations with fixtures, and base64.
- **Executor**:
  - the per-executor environment (`PersistOptions`: user folders, maintenance schedule, migration chain) and the
    per-project store;
  - jobs for open, save, save as and recovery;
  - `onUnsaved: "save"` for the load path and `app.quit`;
  - the `modifiedAt` system transaction;
  - background maintenance (autosave, heartbeats, change detection);
  - VER-02 read-only enforcement;
  - agent file-system roots.
- **Commands**: every `project.*` and `doc.*` command owned by C1.3 is implemented. `c12_registry` asserts that none
  is left with owner `M1 C1.3`. That includes:
  - `project.save` and `project.saveAs`;
  - `doc.object.refs`, `doc.schema.validate`, `doc.validate`, `doc.repair` and `doc.loadReport.get`;
  - `doc.export.json` and `doc.import.json`;
  - `doc.blob.get`, `doc.asset.*` and `doc.package.*`.
- **New-project defaults** (`tt_model::defaults`):
  - a 120-swatch default palette and 8 pens;
  - "Scene-1" with "Camera Peg"/"Camera" and a "Drawing" element.

## Known gaps and residual risks

- **SI-044.** REP-01 says the repair step is undoable. Here it is the history's base and cannot be undone, because
  undoing would install an invalid document. Decision pending with the spec owner.
- **SI-045.** RO-02 asks for a `needs_confirmation` result. C1.3 returns the error `project.changedOnDisk` with both
  choices in its hint. C1.4 owns the confirmation protocol.
- **Windows durability.** Renames are atomic but not write-through (ADR-0014 §3, no unsafe code).
- **Deferred to their owners** (ADR-0014 §16–§17):
  - colour recovery on load (M2 C2.2); until then, unresolved IDs are reported as `colour.unresolved`;
  - zones and other derived data (M2) and thumbnails (M3);
  - the global library and its locks (M9);
  - lint checks reported `unavailable` with their owners.
- **Liveness checks.** Recovery and lock liveness use `tasklist`/`kill -0`. An unknown answer counts as alive, so a
  stale owner can only be taken over after the 5-minute heartbeat timeout.
