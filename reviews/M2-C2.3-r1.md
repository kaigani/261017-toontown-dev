## Findings

### C23-R1-01 — P1: envelope and Reposition All discard zone paint after a disjoint translation

**Requirement:** 02 §7.1.1, §7.1.3–§7.1.4, §8.13; 03 §7.2; SI-070.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked --test c23_r1_commands envelope_translation_keeps_zone_colour_and_maps_its_matrix -- --nocapture` and the same command with filter `reposition_all_maps_zone_matrices_with_geometry`.

Paint a 100×60 rectangle at `(1000,1000)` with the linear gradient, matrix `[100,0,0,60,1000,1000]`. Translate it 300 du right using either a rectangular envelope or `draw.repositionAll.apply`. Both return success. The new zone has **`colourId:null, textureMatrix:null`**, instead of retaining the colour and `[100,0,0,60,1300,1000]`. Ordinary `draw.transform.apply` passes the independent contract variant.

**Fix direction:** generalize the explicit source-zone repaint used by affine `map_objects`. Its repaint branch excludes `Map::Envelope`, and Reposition All bypasses it entirely. Preserve correspondence and mapped fill matrices through settle for both routes, including envelopes whose affine fit is specified by SI-070. Cover nonoverlapping moves, reflections, several drawings and undo.

### C23-R1-02 — P1: point-subset transforms leave other incident anchors behind

**Requirement:** 02 §7.1.2, §3.12; SI-070 shared-junction behavior.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked --test c23_r1_commands point_subset_transform_moves_every_incident_anchor -- --nocapture`.

Two same-layer lines meet at `(1050,1000)`. Transform just the first line's shared anchor by `(10,10)`. Only that line moves. The vertical line retains the old endpoint, and settle creates an unintended crossing at approximately **`(1050,1008.3333333333334)`**, splitting the two original lines into four pieces. Required: both incident anchors reach `(1060,1010)` together, followed by normal re-noding and fill carry-over.

**Fix direction:** expand selected anchors to their same-layer junction equivalence class before mapping, as the separate `draw.point.move` route already attempts. Preserve selection pivots based on the requested subset, rather than allowing incident-stroke expansion to move the pivot.

### C23-R1-03 — P1: Snap to Contour ignores segment interiors

**Requirement:** 02 §7.1.2 and §8.9.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked --test c23_r1_commands snap_to_contour_reaches_a_segment_interior -- --nocapture`.

Move an endpoint from `(1050,1020)` by `(0,-15)` with `snap:true`, toward the middle of a horizontal `(1000,1000)→(1100,1000)` contour. The endpoint remains **`(1050,1005)`**, five du from the contour and inside the eight-du snap radius. Required: snap to `(1050,1000)`, then settle the junction. The handler collects only other strokes' stored anchors, not nearest locations on their segments.

**Fix direction:** include nearest contour points and deterministic ties, retain the shared-anchor rule, and test curved segments and cross-layer boundaries. This is the explicit command flag, independent of the deferred GUI preference.

### C23-R1-04 — P1: Optimize changes composited appearance for a thick pencil under a brush

**Requirement:** 02 §8.2; SI-073.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked --test c23_r1_commands optimize_preserves_opaque_brush_over_thick_lower_pencil -- --nocapture`.

A lower pencil runs `(1000,1000)→(1100,1000)`, thickness 20, in Skin. An upper opaque Ink brush runs `(1010,1008)→(1090,1008)`, width 10. The centre paths are separate, but their rendered areas overlap. Optimize reports **`merged:1`** and changes **2,100 RGBA channels** in a fixed 160×60 render. Required: identical pixels or a declined merge. In one layer all pencils render after brushes; the lower pencil is now above the brush. Coverage pruning also reasons about the centre path rather than its full visible width.

**Fix direction:** evaluate rendered stroke extents, including caps/joins and pencil/brush ordering, before deciding a merge preserves appearance. Keep the existing transparent gradient/texture refusal controls.

### C23-R1-05 — P1: Lines to Brush refuses mapped pencils with a model-schema error

**Requirement:** 02 §8.6; 03 §7.2; SI-068 pencil mappings.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked --test c23_r1_commands lines_to_brush_accepts_a_gradient_pencil_and_keeps_brush_mapping -- --nocapture`.

Give a gradient pencil an explicit `[80,0,0,10,1010,990]` mapping, then convert it. ### C23-R1-06 — P1: tile-off textures extend their border colour outside the mapped image

**Requirement:** 03 §7.4 explicitly requires transparency outside the once-stretched image. SI-074 governs orientation and does not change this extent rule.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-render --locked --test c23_r1_texture -- --nocapture`.

Fill a 30×30 shape with a nonrepeating red image mapped only onto pixel rectangle `(10,10)–(20,20)`, over transparent background. Both `(15,15)` and **`(5,5)` return `[255,0,0,255]`**. The latter must be `[0,0,0,0]`. The adapter selects `Extend::Pad`, which repeats edge pixels; no mapped-image clip is applied.

**Fix direction:** enforce a transparent extent outside the transformed unit square for tile-off images, with rotated/skewed extent tests. Retain bilinear sampling and SI-074's upright convention inside the image.

### C23-R1-07 — P1: Smooth can hold the command call beyond the watchdog

**Requirement:** plan §3.2/§5.2 never-block; 02 §13.1.12; bounded command work.

**Reproduce:** `python tests/adversarial/c23-r1-smooth.py --binary target/c23-r1-ci/headless-Windows/toontown-headless.exe --out reviews/evidence/M2-C2.3-r1` after downloading the declared CI artifact.

On the exact-head CI release executable, add an invisible `fit:false` path of **2,000 distinct collinear anchors**, then smooth at tolerance 1. Creation succeeds. The Smooth MCP call provides **no response before an external 40-second timeout**; observed wait **40.001911 seconds**. The process is stopped by the SDK context cleanup. [Raw observation](evidence/M2-C2.3-r1/smooth-never-block.json), [SDK failure log](evidence/M2-C2.3-r1/smooth-never-block.log).

`ops::smooth::smooth` repeatedly evaluates every removable anchor, constructs original-span samples and computes bidirectional distances, then removes one anchor per pass. It has no charged work account or cancellation check. The executor handle's 30-second receive timeout does not interrupt the worker. The MCP route then synchronously requests activity auditing on that worker before returning its answer, explaining why a caller timeout need not yield a prompt MCP response. Small dense-distance controls pass; this is a work-bounding defect, not a demonstrated tolerance defect.

**Fix direction:** charge aggregate simplification work, propagate a typed `limit.exceeded` before the watchdog, or use a bounded algorithm/job with cancellation. Verify no partial commit and that a queued read/maintenance task still answers. The flatten work account does not bound Smooth.

### C23-R1-08 — P1: new Cutter/Optimize predicates reintroduce floating-point crossing decisions

**Ruling:** exact i128 predicates for every crossing/vertex decision; ADR-0017 and the C2.2 exact-classification rulings.

**Reproduce:** `cargo test -p tt-vector --locked --test c23_r1_geometry cutter_overlap_classification_keeps_exact_grid_crossings -- --nocapture`.

Let `N=500000000`, `a=N/64`, `b=(N−1)/64`, `c=(N−2)/64`. Segments `(-a,-b)→(a,b)` and `(-b,-c)→(b,c)` cross at zero. Their exact grid determinant is **−1**, while the floating calculation is **0**. `ops::cut::polylines_cross` returns **false**. Coordinates are inside the supported range and exactly on the snap grid.

The new `inside` and `segments_cross` helpers use f64 cross products; layer coverage/merge decisions and selection geometry call related floating predicates. The main planar engine's repaired predicate remains intact, but these newly introduced decision paths bypass it.

**Fix direction:** reuse the exact grid classification consistently in these helpers, including endpoint, shared-edge and containment decisions. Retain the old determinant and endpoint regressions; add the new operations to that corpus.
