## 5. The static light table (C5.2a kernel, C5.2b panel)

- **Saved with the scene** (04 §15.3): the per-scene items are written in the project's session data and restored on
  open; items whose drawing, element or scene is gone are dropped on read (they already are on list). Storage follows
  SI-085 (two storage models) and SI-126: the decision stays the user's; C5.2 keeps 02 §12.3's scene scope and records
  what it chose in SI-126.
- **The panel** (C5.2b) in the Exposure Sheet: an ordered list, each item with a visibility control, an over / under
  control and the label `<drawing> in <element>`; drag to reorder; the item and panel commands of 15.4 in contexts;
  cells dragged onto it add them (12.9). The Drawing view's aid section keeps its controls (they send the
  `drawAid.staticLightTable.*` commands of the same state) or gives them up for the panel (Q4).

## 6. The undo history's memory bound (E-51, first in C5.2a)

- Each step's size measured from its patch (`EntryCommit` undo and redo patches: the serialised size of their values,
  computed once when the step is pushed; a snapshot step: the entries it does not share with the live document).
- `History::push` discards the oldest steps first while the total exceeds the bound, never below 200 steps (01 §6.1's
  floor); the 1,000-step limit stays. When 200 steps alone exceed the bound they are kept and stderr says so once per
  history ("the undo history holds N MB in its last 200 steps, over the bound").
- **The bound's value:** 01 §7.3 gives no preference for it (`display.cacheMemoryPercent` is the display caches'), so a
  fixed **1 GiB**, recorded as **SI-217** for the user.
- The clean marker and the floor follow a discard as they follow the step-count discard today (`history.rs` `push`).
- **Search rule first:** reviewer tests on history depth, clean markers after discards, `edit.history.get` and the
  `history` MCP tool.
- **Tests:** the bound holds on a history of large steps (the keyed-peg steps of the §6.2 budget scene, or synthetic
  patches of a known size); undo and redo stay correct across a discard (the document after undo all equals the state
  at the oldest kept step); the floor is never crossed (200 steps over the bound are all kept, and the one stderr line);
  a discarded step cannot be reached by `edit.history.get`, `edit.undo {steps}` or the MCP `history` tool. The key-list
  diff by frame stays the M11 C11.1 ticket.

## 7. Thumbnails (C5.2a kernel, C5.2b window)

- **Kernel:** the derived `thumbnail` of a drawing (and the project's) leaves `derived.rs`'s unavailable list:
  `doc.object.get {derived: ["thumbnail"]}` answers a small PNG; the same renderer as `xsheet.exposures.get
  {include: ["thumbnails"]}` (64 px) and `element.drawing.list {thumbnails}` (16 to 512 px, synchronous today). A cache
  keyed by the drawing's id and revision and the size, bounded (its share of `display.cacheMemoryPercent`).
- **Window:** 12.3 asks for thumbnails "computed in the background with a progress indication". The window asks for
  the visible cells' thumbnails in batches after it has drawn the cells without them, shows a progress line while
  batches are out, and draws each as it arrives; a scroll cancels the batches out of view. Where the rendering runs
  (the document thread per batch, or a job) is Q3; the budget is §11's.

## 8. The Exposure Sheet panel (C5.2b)

The components of 04 §12.2: the toolbar (quick-add of the five cell-based types, thumbnails toggle, light-table panel
toggle, element list toggle, scene selector, a control opening the context commands); the element list panel (12.6:
the cell-based elements in Exposure Sheet order, visibility in the sheet `element.visibility.set {scope: "xsheet"}`,
drag to reorder `element.order.set {order: "xsheet"}`, selection); the frame-number column (frames or feet and frames,
the current frame highlighted); the column headers (name, type icon, foreground / background and annotation
indicators, colour marker, note indicator; rename in place on a double click, as the Timeline's (E-50); context per
column type 12.8; border drag `xsheet.columnWidth.set`; header drag reorders); the grid (cells per 12.3: drawing names
or `<element>-<drawing>`, held cells by text or hold line, thumbnails, lock and note indicators, sound waveforms and
mouth letters read-only until M8, media thumbnails; selection highlight, focus rectangle, current-frame row); the
light-table panel (§5). Virtualised in both directions (rows and columns in view plus a margin), read with
`xsheet.exposures.get` for the window in view, behind read gates, with the C5.1b read discipline (reads only what a
notice changes, one heavy read in flight, the subscription made once per engine). Selection and navigation of 12.5:
click, extend and toggle, column-header click, arrows (frame, element), previous / next drawing, previous / next element
(`element.current.move`), the in-cell editor on typing or a double click (`cell.label.set`, a new name
`element.drawing.create`, Q1), deselect-all clearing the cell selection.
