## 3. What C5.1b built

### 3.1 The Timeline panel

The panel (`desktop/src/timeline/TimelinePanel.tsx`) and its pure modules (`model`, `geometry`, `draw`, `gesture`,
`menus`, `rows`): the layer list (virtualised; disclosure for rows with children, 04 line 605; visibility; rename in
place), the ruler and the tracks (canvas), its toolbar (Add, Parent Peg, Solo as a toggle, Show All, Drag Options,
Zoom), the horizontal scroll bar, the row, cell and ruler context menus with their dialogs, the Element and Play menus,
the main toolbar's Play/Stop and Last frame. Every read behind a read gate; every gesture through the kernel's
interpreter (`timing_gesture` IPC: press, preview, release, abort). The arrow keys move the current cell (plan §6.5);
the keymap default gains `/` for Show Timeline Selection (SI-216).

What the panel reads again, and when (the engine runs one command at a time, so a read the draw does not need delays
the one it does; found by the window's budgets on the plan's §6.2 scene, §5):

- a frame change moves the playhead; an app-state change of fields the rows do not depend on (frame, element, drawing,
  selection, mode, view, focus, tool, playback) reads the selection alone; any other field reads everything;
- a view notice that moves the view alone sets it, and the rows are read for the new frames; a change of its other
  fields (display toggles, reveal, scene) reads everything (E-40);
- one `timeline.rows.list` in flight: a newer request makes the answer in flight stale and is sent when it returns;
- the event subscription is made once per engine (it subscribed again, and read everything twice, at each change of
  the frames in view).
