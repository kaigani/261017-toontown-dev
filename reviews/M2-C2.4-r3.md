## Findings

### C24-R3-02 — P1: Vectorize's comparison accepts a non-corresponding mask boundary

**Clause/ruling:** C24-R2-01; C2.3 round-3 readiness item 2; 02 §3.12 and §11.2; the charter's independently predicted changed boundaries and canonical zone identities for every topology-changing route.

**Reproduction:** `cargo test --release -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c24_r3_domains vectorize_comparison_rejects_a_wrong_boundary_with_the_right_area_and_probe -- --exact --nocapture`. The initial [compiled debug execution](evidence/M2-C2.4-r3/vectorize-mutant-debug.txt) already reproduces the failure. The new file retains the builder's domain property and adds a separate mutant control; no original test is edited.

**Observed versus required:** the copied comparison from `c24_route_domains.rs::vectorize_masks_enclose_the_predicted_paper_components` accepts `Ok(())` for a 5×5 mask with one central paper pixel, when the output's hole is the rectangle `[240,960] × [560,640]` du. The expected pixel is `[480,720]²` du at the established 1200-du width. Both have area 57,600 du²; the wrong rectangle's published interior point still falls inside the permitted probe region. Its boundary reaches a full pixel beyond the expected boundary, exceeding even the half-pixel geometric envelope suggested by the property. A real command-created brush supplies this self-consistent wrong result, including zones, boundaries and IDs.

The semantic stage checks component count, an interior point and a broad area allowance; it never compares the mask's boundary with the produced boundary. The exact stage then builds its expected map from that produced outline and verifies the product against it. This independently verifies output-map consistency, but cannot establish correspondence to the input mask. The control's failure is that the verifier accepts the mutant; it does **not** demonstrate that the shipped Vectorize command creates this wrong rectangle.

The additional enabled `vectorize_comparison_rejects_a_wrong_boundary_at_the_correct_image_origin` uses the specified extent `[-600,600]²`, predicted paper pixel `[-120,120]²`, and wrong hole `[-360,360] × [-40,40]`. It also receives `Ok(())` in release. Thus the failure is independent of centering; [release domain log](evidence/M2-C2.4-r3/domains-4096.txt). Both controls stay failing.

**Fix direction:** independently trace the input mask's paper/ink boundaries in the fixed source-to-drawing mapping; match the fitted output boundaries and their adjacency/holes to those predictions within the documented fitting/snapping envelope. Retain the exact independent map/ID stage and this enabled rejection control. Do not substitute area/count agreement for boundary correspondence. The other route-domain improvements are assessed separately below; this remaining route suffices to keep R2-01 open.

For scoped closure, the orchestrator must explicitly authorize any mirroring of the repaired builder comparator into the reviewer-owned `r3_shipped_vectorize_comparison` helper. Declare those helper hunks and keep both new `#[test]` bodies byte-identical. The round-2 helper exception does not automatically authorize a round-3 reviewer-file edit.
