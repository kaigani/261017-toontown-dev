## 4. D-50: the delays and the six states

- **Turning the delays on:**
  1. Build with `cargo build --release -p tt-desktop --features test-hooks`.
  2. Launch with `TT_TEST_COMMIT_DELAY_MS=<ms>`. This delays a user gesture commit's answer; the commit itself is made
     at once.
  3. Add `TT_TEST_SCENE_DELAY_MS=<ms>` to delay a scene answer.

  Both variables are read once at launch (`desktop/src-tauri/src/delay_hooks.rs`). A product build has neither:
  `scripts/check-release-features.py` passes, and the hooks binary is 74 KB larger than the product binary.
- **The check:** `python scripts/c43-preview-lifecycle.py <hooks toontown> <toontown-headless> <out>`. Defaults are
  commit 2500 ms and scene 1800 ms. Real OS input in the Drawing view. Each state ends with the window's drawing exactly
  equal to a headless engine's drawing after the window's recorded commands were sent directly (D-32).
- **Result: 18 of 18 rows pass.**
  - **(a)** For Select move, Select corner scale, Brush and Rectangle, the preview or overlay was drawn on every sample
    until the answer (about 33 samples, answer at about 2.56 s) and on none from 1 s after it.
  - **(b)** A scene was requested just before the press. The release came 1.12 s later, and the scene arrived 0.68 s
    after the release: after the commit, before its answer. The preview held until the committed frame.
    - An agent command sent during the drag waits for the end of the user's gesture, so it cannot stage this order.
  - **(c)** A real Ctrl+Z right after the release: nothing is left drawn, and the drawing equals the commands then undo.
  - **(d)** The second press came 0.56 s after the first release; both committed.
  - **(e)** A locked drawing: refused with `drawing.locked`, nothing drawn from 0.2 s after the answer, the drawing
    unchanged.
  - **(f)** The Camera view and back while the answer was pending.

## 6. D-49 item 4: `job_wait` on a large completed job

**Reproduced and explained** in about 25 of the 60 minutes allowed. The job: `render.range.run` 1..12000 at 0.05.

- **Over stdio, headless:** 3 of 3 waits answered: while the job ran, after it completed, and with a progress token
  (12 004 notifications). The answer is 4.1 MB: the job's `result` lists every frame.
- **Over HTTP (the window):** the first try failed with "SSE stream ended without a response".
- **Cause, from the SDK debug log:** `httpx2.SSEError: Server-sent event exceeded the 1048576 byte limit`.
  - httpx2 2.12.0 caps one event at 1 MiB.
  - `mcp` 2.0.0 catches every exception in `_handle_sse_response` and resolves the request as "stream ended".
  - The server wrote the whole answer.
- **For the orchestrator:** a job record of that size exceeds a common client's event cap. Whether job results should
  be bounded is a product question.
