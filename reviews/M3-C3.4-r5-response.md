## 1b. Open: the reviewer's new `c34_r5_deadlines::queued_job_wait_step_uses_enqueue_deadline` fails on macOS

Not changed in this response, because the ruling bars changes to waits and deadlines here. Raised for the finalize
check.

- **The test:** a plain sequential batch with a 1000 ms budget, queued for about 700 ms behind a held action. Its
  `job.wait` step is bounded by budget - queued time - margin (a tenth of the budget, 100 ms), and the batch must
  answer with both steps within 1000 ms.
- **Results at this head (`6ffb5c2`, run 43315096638):** on macOS `check` it failed in attempts 1 and 3, each time
  with the caller's own `command.timeout` ("`batch` did not answer within 1000 ms"). Attempt 2 stopped earlier on an
  unrelated M0 front-end lint timeout (`c01-r2-boundary`, a 30 s `spawnSync`). It passed on macOS at `e688138` (run
  43308576107), on Windows in every run, and 10 of 10 times locally on Windows. Since the code is identical, this is
  scheduling on the loaded macOS runner.
- **Likely cause:** the 100 ms left after the wait must cover the rest of the step, `doc.state.get` and delivery of
  the reply, while the test's job thread spins at 20 ms per work charge. The earlier round-4 macOS failures of
  `c34_r1_contract` had the same shape.
- **Options for the orchestrator:**
  1. Authorize a narrow wait change, for example a post-wait margin with an absolute floor (such as max(budget/10,
     150 ms)), with the ADR text and a builder test.
  2. Have the reviewer give the test more headroom (a longer budget).
  3. Route the test to a release job by exact name, body unchanged, like the other timing loops.
