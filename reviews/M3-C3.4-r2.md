## Findings in adjudication

### C34-R2-01 — P1 — an already-cancelled invocation can commit

Clause: request-owned cancellation, ADR-0020 decision 8 and the round-2 commit/cancel charter. Permanent reproduction: `c34_r2_cancellation::already_cancelled_mutation_never_commits`. Pass a probe that is already true to `invoke_served_until` for `element.create` on a fresh fixture. Required: no change or undo/event publication after cancellation has won. Observed: success with a complete change envelope and an undo record; document revision advances. `call_until` enqueues without checking the probe and checks it only following a receive timeout; a fast reply bypasses it entirely. Fix direction: establish cancellation acceptance before enqueue and arbitrate cancellation against commit, preserving remembered outcomes and retries. Polling alone is not an acceptance boundary. The test fails by design at the reviewed head.

### C34-R2-02 — P2 — direct job.wait ignores caller cancellation

Clause: request cancellation must stop waiting while leaving the job independently owned; ADR-0020 decision 8 and the explicitly requested direct-job.wait check. Permanent reproduction: `c34_r2_cancellation::direct_job_wait_observes_cancellation_probe`. Start a range job, then call direct `job.wait` with a true probe and 500 ms timeout. Required: `command.timeout`, `details.reason = cancelled`, retained jobId, early return. Observed: the probe is never called; the ordinary job result or wait timeout wins. The early return in `invoke_served_until` calls `job_wait(inv)` without the probe; both its begin/finish calls and `jobs.wait` omit it. Fix direction: make the direct and attached-retry wait paths cancellation-aware, preserving requestId ownership and settlement. This is bounded waiting, not job cancellation. The test settles the job before asserting and fails by design.
