## CI finding during the response (R1-14 follow-up)

The first CI run of the round-2 code (41682281474) failed `check (windows-latest)` on the reviewer test
`c31_r1_selection_budget` (unchanged since round 1, passing locally and in run 41647392918): with a 200-ms caller
budget, a 100 000-cell `cell.select` followed by `doc.state.get`, and the read timed out. Its cause is not a round-2
change. Measured locally (debug), the select's charged work took 22 ms, but after it the handler re-read the whole
selection (a copy and one allocation per cell, 28 ms) and the executor's uncharged post-call settle took about 115 ms
(context repair re-validating the same list three times through the selection, its context and the mirror: 68 ms;
the state events: 30 ms; the selection-event payload: 25 ms). On a runner about twice as slow that work outlasts the
next caller's budget. Fixes: `app::cell_ref_parts` parses cell references without allocating; the repair checks each
shared list once (by pointer) and memoises the element per run of references; `selected_cells` and
`cell_selection_state` read the list borrowed (`AppContext::selection_list`). A 100 000-cell selection now costs 88 ms
end to end locally (was 175 ms), of which the post-call settle is about 48 ms, most of it the `app.state.changed` and
`edit.selection.changed` payloads, which carry the ids by contract. `c31_r1_selection_budget` 5/5 repeated runs;
ADR-0015 history updated. The run's three cancelled macOS jobs were not acquired by a hosted runner (capacity
annotation), not test failures.
