### C31-R1-01 — P1 — Columnate can return fewer frames than explicitly requested

- **Clause:** 04 §13.10, total-frames computation and truncation; `cell.columnate.apply` result contract.
- **Reproduction:** `cargo test -p tt-anim --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_oracle columnate_total_frames_short_last_reaches_requested_length`; also `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_contract columnate_exact_total_with_short_final_hold`.
- **Observed:** first/last drawings `1`/`10` (three bank entries), continuous, `exposeEach:4`, `exposeLast:1`, `totalFrames:12` produces 9 frames and one loop. The pure helper returns `[(1,4),(2,4),(3,1)]`.
- **Required:** produce the requested 12 frames under the total-frames rule; a truncated final loop must not leave a shortfall caused by applying `exposeLast` before satisfying that count.
- **Fix direction:** define total-frames precedence explicitly and compute enough sequence before truncation. Cover unequal each/last values, exact loop boundaries, n=1, both loop modes and the no-loop case.

### C31-R1-02 — P1 — New TimelineState escapes dry-run and atomic rollback

- **Ruling:** ADR-0013 §5–6; ADR-0015 history C23-R3-01 acceptance boundary; binding M2 handoff items 1 and 7.
- **Reproduction:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_contract timeline_colour`; `cargo test -p tt-mcp --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_protocol generic_tool_dry_run_does_not_leak_category_colour`.
- **Observed:** dry-running `element.typeColour.set {category:"pegs",colour:"#123456"}` changes subsequent `element.typeColour.get` from `#C9A0DCFF` to `#123456FF`. An atomic batch containing that write followed by an invalid rename also leaves the colour changed.
- **Required:** discarded invocations and failed groups restore every session/UI field, as well as the document, history and effects.
- **Cause/fix direction:** `Session.timeline` was added without corresponding state in `run::Rollback` or `cx::Savepoint`. Include the whole state in both snapshots and in cancellation acceptance regression tests. Solo and future light-table writes share this omission; their lack of document revisions is not rollback coverage.

### C31-R1-03 — P1 — Area events and AG-02 equivalence are incomplete

- **Clauses:** 10 AG-02 and §2.8 `x-command`; 04 §10.2, §17.4–§17.5; ADR-0013 §2.
- **Reproduction:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_contract delegated_`; run `patch_unlock_ops_matches_command` and `automatic_position_changes_emit_selection_event` in the same test binary.
- **Observed:** patching `visibleInXsheet:false` leaves a hidden element current, unlike `element.visibility.set`. Patching a note emits no `element.state.changed`. RFC 6902 `/locked:false` refuses on a locked drawing (`drawing.locked`), while the equivalent `props` unlock works. Direct element creation and automatic current-element movement on hiding do not emit `cell.selection.changed`.
- **Bank-event controls:** `multi_bank_named_insert_emits_only_each_banks_created_ids` shows that named insertion into Bob and Notes reports both banks' created IDs in Bob's event and omits Notes' event. `sheet_and_label_creation_emit_drawing_bank_events` shows that create-missing sheet writes emit no drawing-bank creation event (the label path has the same missing producer in code).
- **Required:** delegated writable properties apply the owning command's complete behavior, including UI consequences and exactly the appropriate area event. Current-position/row-selection changes are observable regardless of which command caused them. The handoff's explicit `.current.set/.move` fix covers those two commands only.
- **Fix direction:** share semantic validation/application/event helpers with the owning commands for all seven delegated families, including RFC 6902 forms. Audit automatic selection/current-position producers and every drawing-creation path; coalesce drawing IDs by bank and emit to the correct clone-group users. Keep non-delegated properties refusing with their actual owner and avoid duplicate events.

### C31-R1-04 — P1 — Cell and whole-element clipboards do not carry source artwork

- **Clauses:** 04 §13.16, §14.1; 01 clipboard bridges; handoff item 6.
- **Reproduction:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_contract clipboard_`; also `whole_element_clipboard_cross_project_contains_banks`.
- **Observed:** copy Bob's cells, delete Bob (last bank user), then paste into a new drawing element: `cell.clipboardEmpty`, “a copied drawing no longer exists.” Opening a distinct seeded project after copying cells produces the same refusal. Whole-element copy/paste across projects refuses because the source bank is absent.
- **Required:** a copy contains the drawings/properties necessary for a later paste, including into another project. Removing or replacing the source document must not invalidate captured artwork.
- **Cause/fix direction:** `store_cells` stores element IDs, offsets and drawing references only; whole-element items also retain external bank references. Capture a typed, self-contained payload with bank/drawing content, assets and needed references. Remap IDs into the destination, preserve drawing-local owner/stretch records and opaque namespaces, and verify both generic and `cell.*` bridges.

### C31-R1-05 — P1 — Cell notes are neither copied nor shifted with cells

- **Clauses:** 04 §7.2, §13 general shift rule, §13.6–§13.8 and §13.16 (“labels, drawings, properties”); 10 §4.7.
- **Reproduction:** run `copied_cells_keep_notes` and `inserted_cells_shift_existing_notes` in `c31_r1_contract`.
- **Observed:** copying frames 1–3 with a note at frame 1 and pasting at frame 50 leaves no note at 50. Inserting two blank cells before a note at frame 9 leaves the note at 9 instead of 11.
- **Required:** cell transfers carry cell properties and shifting cells moves those properties consistently. Notes are element/frame data, independent of drawing identity.
- **Fix direction:** carry/re-time `cellNotes` alongside exposure edits, including inserts, removals, cycle/columnate and overlapping moves; define overwrite/clear behavior and make each edit one transaction. Keep drawing/zone revisions unchanged.

### C31-R1-07 — P1 — Overlapping multi-column transfer reads its own earlier writes

- **Clauses:** 04 §13 general per-element rule, §13.16; `cell.range.move` source/destination contract.
- **Reproduction:** `cargo test -p tt-executor --locked -j 4 --test c31_r1_contract multi_column_overlap_copies_original_source_values`.

- **Required:** a bulk transfer captures the original selected source cells once, then applies them to the destinations atomically. A successful copy must not depend on iteration direction.
- **Fix direction:** separate immutable source snapshots from mutable destination columns and maintain a command-wide drawing mapper. Cover forward/backward overlap, same/shared/distinct banks and all four transfer modes.
