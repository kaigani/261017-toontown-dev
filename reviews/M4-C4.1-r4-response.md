## 1. CI on `88ac1b5` (run 43554004796)

- **`check (macos-latest)`** failed in `cargo test --workspace` on the reviewer-owned M3 test
  `crates/executor/tests/c34_r1_contract.rs::sequential_wait_timeout_retains_job_identity` (`Error: "step envelopes
  required"`: the batch, under a 500 ms budget, came back as a call-level timeout instead of a step envelope). It had
  failed the same way in the first attempt of run 43548393377 and passed in that run's rerun; it never failed on
  Windows, also with 48 copies in parallel. **Cause found in my round-2 code:** ruling O-18 made the recovery heartbeat
  take the process-wide recovery ownership lock, and the heartbeat runs on the document thread. Any engine's autosave
  write in the same process (in that test binary the other test's engine runs in parallel) held the lock for the length
  of a write on a slow disk, and the document thread waited behind it, long enough to push the batch past its
  budget. **Fix:** the heartbeat never waits for that lock: while a write or a release holds it, that heartbeat is
  skipped (a recovery write refreshes `heartbeatAt` itself; the next heartbeat comes a minute later)
  (`tt_persist::recovery::heartbeat_owned`, `try_lock`). Writes and releases keep holding the lock across their step
  (O-18 unchanged).
- **`desktop gui (windows-latest)`** failed in the reviewer's round-2 float probe
  (`tests/adversarial/c41-r2-desktop-floats.py`), check **`move activity settles`**; every other check of the probe (26)
  and every other step passed, including the OS move write-back (`OS move written back`: the float at (260, 230), not
  clamped). **What differs on the runner is timing, not placement:** the check compares the `window.panel.set` entries
  among the **100 newest** activity entries, read at the middle and at the end of a 2-second loop of
  `agent.activity.list` calls. Every agent call is appended to the activity log (11 §11), the reads included, so the
  loop's own calls push older entries out of the 100-entry window. The receipt shows it: `before` holds `seq` 4 and 7,
  `after` is empty. On the runner the loop made enough calls between the middle and the end to push them out; on the
  workstation it did not. The activity log records agent calls only, so the check cannot see host writes at all (a
  feedback loop of host `window.panel.set` would not appear in it). This is not a product race, and the product
  follows the spec here (every agent call is logged); I have not changed the probe. **I ask for a ruling on the check
  itself** (for example comparing entries after a `seq` cursor, or `agent.activity.list` with a filter). It passed in
  run 43548393377 on `cd06751`, locally (three runs) and in the reviewer's live run; if it recurs on the final head I
  rerun once and report it.
