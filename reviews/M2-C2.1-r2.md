## New findings

### C21-R2-01 — P1: spatial deduplication loses unsmoothed pressure extrema

`crates/vector/src/fit.rs:145`, `:210–228` (`collapse`), consumed by `crates/vector/src/stroke.rs:64–68`. Fit distinct samples `(0,0,p=1)`, `(0.001,0,p=0)` at smoothness 0, round pen min=0/max=100. Spatial dedupe averages pressure to 0.5 and returns a dab with `[[0,.5],[1,.5]]`. Outline maximum y is **25.005063129**, although the starting tip is a radius-50 disk: about 25 du of missing swept area, independent of positional tolerance.

Spec: 02 §3.3 steps 1/5 (drop positional duplicates; pressure smoothing follows Smoothness), §3.2 pressure map, §3.4 locally sized swept-tip union; ADR-0016 §§4–5. Preserve pressure extrema/steps or their swept area independently of positional dedupe. Placing all surviving knots cannot restore already averaged pressure.

Permanent regression: `crates/vector/tests/c21_r2_geometry.rs::spatial_dedupe_preserves_pressure_extrema_for_the_brush_sweep`. Debug and release fail: [debug](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/geometry-final.log), [release](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/geometry-release.log). Reopens R1-02 through another input path.

### C21-R2-02 — P1: NFC fallback applies the first run's format to unrelated text

`crates/vector/src/text.rs:295–340`, especially `:330–334`. Create `"\u0995\u09C7Z"` (Bengali KA, vowel sign E, Latin Z), make only Z bold, then insert `"\u09BE"` at code-point index 2. NFC correctly produces `"\u0995\u09CBZ"`, but the committed result is one unbold run of length 3. Z did not participate in composition. These spacing vowel signs have combining class zero; normalizing the implementation's pieces differs from normalizing the whole string, so the fallback assigns everything to `lengths[0]` and drops later formats.

Spec: 02 §3.7 per-character rich text and §7.6 editing; 10 §1.1 NFC, §4.9 code-point ranges; ADR-0016 §6 starter-run ownership. Valid ranges do not guarantee preserved rich-text meaning. Remap all unaffected characters across CCC=0 compositions/canonical reordering instead of a whole-text first-run fallback.

Permanent regressions: `crates/vector/tests/c21_r2_geometry.rs::nfc_composition_preserves_unaffected_character_formats` and `crates/executor/tests/c21_r2_contracts.rs::unrelated_format_survives_nfc_in_command_path`. The latter proves committed command/read-back behavior: [contracts](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/contracts-final.log). Both final probes include the consonant for an ordinary Bengali syllable and are rerun in release.

### C21-R2-03 — P1: placement count does not bound complete brush work

`crates/vector/src/brush.rs:157–270`, reached from `stroke.rs::build` and `::rederive`. Straight open spine `(0,0) → (100,0)`, round pen min=0/max=100, **99,000** ordered knots `[i/98999, i%2]`: below the placement cap, but `outline` still runs at 30 seconds. The parent terminates and reaps the child. Reproduces in both optimized-dependency debug and **release** (30.28/30.34 s parent observations), with no injected non-returning handler or test hook.

Spec: binding obligation 11 (bounded geometry work/autosave responsiveness), 02 §§3.4/13.1, ADR-0013 serialized-worker/watchdog limitation, ADR-0016 §5 bounded-work claim. MAX_PLACEMENTS limits one count, not total union/bridge/densification/fitting work; line 270 calls unbounded `fit::fit_with`. The timing proves the complete geometry call exceeded the watchdog, not which internal phase consumed every second. It runs before staging; a caller timeout cannot free the occupied document worker. Budget the complete operation or refuse promptly with limit.exceeded, including large pressure maps. The final rerun's release parent observation is **30.41 s**; debug is **30.27 s**.

Permanent regression: `crates/vector/tests/c21_r2_geometry.rs::many_pressure_knots_brush_respects_watchdog`; its ordinary test launches an ignored worker with a bounded timeout. [Focused debug](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/brush-budget.log), [full debug](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/geometry-final.log), [release](evidence/M2-C2.1-r2/geometry-release.log).
