AVX2, NEON and full-corpus AVX-512 evidence exists; SSE4.2-only hardware remains untested.
- **Opacity and dependency obligations:** retain the documented u8 opacity limitation; matching hashes are not a correctness proof. Explicitly accept vello's transitive maintenance/unsafe exposure using the corrected locked dependency audit. The old subdivision `powf` example was test-only; no specific libm cause of scalar/SIMD differences was established.
- **Cost and export budgets:** use the corrected measured f32 trade-off of approximately **−3% to +15%**, not a bound. The **55/38/146 ms at 1080p** scalar-f32/SIMD-f32/ttraster figures are area extrapolations, not output-size measurements or export budgets. M3 needs production-density rendering, effects, memory, cancellation and codec measurements. Historical encode measurements remain tied to their named revisions; R3 measured structural-validation overhead separately.
- **Hardware floor:** real user Mac, macOS/WebKit floor, Retina/multiple displays, ProMotion and weaker GPUs remain unmeasured. Newer virtualized Mac CI does not replace that coverage. Keep the original C0.2 physical-pen/receiving-boundary and Mac manual obligations under their existing rulings.
- **Timing interpretation:** WebGPU completion latency includes queue work and callback scheduling; it is not GPU timestamp duration. WebGL2 supplies no GPU duration. - **Residency and transport:** measured full-list transfer/decode costs support resident geometry and bounded changes, but group transforms in the spike are generated locally in JS. M4 needs measured host deltas, stable identities/revisions, ownership/lifetime, batching/backpressure, cache eviction, invalidation, resync and gesture overlays while keeping one authoritative logical render list for interactive and CPU renderers.

Compare reduced-performance/tiled host bitmaps with webview software rendering end to end. Native wgpu child-window composition, input and resize integration remain an unbuilt hypothesis.

Neither current adapter is spec-complete; extend correctness and determinism goldens before adoption.
