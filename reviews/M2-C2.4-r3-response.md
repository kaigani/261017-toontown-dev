**C24-R3-02 details.** `trace_mask` traces the mask with the documented rules, rewritten independently of the
product's tracer:
- ink 8-connected, paper 4-connected;
- unit edges with the ink on their right, and a left turn at a saddle;
- outer boundaries and holes told apart by area sign;
- contours through edge midpoints, with the image width spanning 1200 du, centred, y up.

`vectorize_comparison` then requires:
- one brush stroke per ink region, whose bridged outline (bridges cancelled) gives exactly that region's traced
  contours, each within 0.25 + 0.5 × 3 du plus flattening and a grid cell, both ways;
- one unpainted zone per enclosed paper component, inside that component's traced hole and outside its islands, bounded
  by exactly the enclosing region's and the islands' strokes, with its area within the perimeter × that envelope;
- traced holes and a flood fill agreeing on the number of enclosed components;
- the exact snap-rounded map and ID stage on the output.
