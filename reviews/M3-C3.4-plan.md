## 2. Independent expectations (computed in the script, never read from the product)

- **Positions:** the script implements 05 §9.1-9.2 itself: a Kochanek-Bartels spline (t = c = b = 0) with reflected
  phantom ends, 64 samples per segment, cumulative chord length, and linear parameter interpolation between samples.
  It also implements 05 §10.4-10.5: V(f) pinned at the keys' arc lengths and shaped by the presets' normative Bézier
  control points (x(u) = t solved by bisection). Expected agreement is 1e-9 field. A second check bounds the sampling's
  effect against the exact eased distance (1e-3 field).
- **Rendered centres:** each of the 24 frames is decoded (a pure-Python PNG reader) and the ball's coverage-weighted
  centroid is measured. It is compared with 960 + 160·x, 540 − 160·y (1 field = 1920 / 12 pixels at the default
  camera, SI-105; 6 / tan(FOV/2) = 16, 05 §2.11) within 0.05 px. The variant adds the parent rotation/scale and the
  camera roll (positive roll is counter-clockwise, 05 §1.4).
- **Extrema, holds:** the floor at 12, the top at 1 and 24, frames 25-30 hold, monotonic fall and rise, slow start and
  fastest at the floor.
- **Contact sheet:** columns ⌈√24⌉ = 5, cell 240 x 135, 4-px gaps, a 14-px label band (SI-108), giving the expected
  cell rectangles and the sheet size; every cell must equal the frame rendered at the cell size pixel for pixel;
  every label band holds ink, and the 24 labels differ.
- **Sequence:** names, order, count, dimensions (PNG header and manifest), SHA-256 and byte count of every file, fps 24,
  duration 1.0 s, range 1-24.
