## 1. Findings

**C42A-R2-01 and C42A-R2-02 (P2), the common addressing parameters** (02 §13.1 item 5).
- **Commands:** every point-taking `draw.*` command now declares `space` (`drawing`, `scene`, `view`; default
  `drawing`), `view` and `frame`.
- **Conversion:** one normaliser in the command pipeline (`crate::addressing`, before the precheck and the handler)
  converts their view-space points with the view's one mapping. Points use the whole affine; vectors and relative handles
  use its linear part. A rectangle is converted only without rotation; in a rotated view it answers `arg.invalid`
  ("give a polygon"). Scene space answers `arg.invalid` until the Camera view (as the colour commands do). `view` stays
  in the parameters for the tolerances.
- **Gestures:**
  - The Polyline gesture states `view` when the zoom is not 100 %.
  - The Select click states `view` when the hit tolerance decides the outcome: the zoom is not 100 % and what lies under
    the exact point differs from what the tolerance reaches.
  - When `view` is omitted, the command without it gives the identical result (no view means 100 %, and an exact hit
    needs no tolerance).
- **Why this rule:** it keeps every permanent test unchanged. Round 1's `zoom`/`select`/`polyline` cases (at 100 %) and
  round 2's `c1_move` (a click inside a zone at zoom 4) expect no `view`; `c1_click_view_tolerance` and
  `b1_transformed_lasso_selection_and_eraser` expect it. If `view` should always be stated, those earlier tests would
  need amending.

**The audit (complete, mechanical):**

1. **Parameters.** `scripts/check-addressing-params.py`, a new step in `lint`, derives "point-taking" from the generated
   schemas: a top-level parameter that is a point, an array of points, or an object holding points one level down. It
   fails when any of `space` (with its three values), `view` or `frame` is missing. 27 `draw.*`/`colour.*` commands are
   checked, all comply.
   - `draw.drawing.render` is exempt, with the reason in the script: its `region` is a thumbnail's area, not a point
     target.
   - The audit found `draw.pointSelection.set` and `draw.hitTest.get`, both now included.
2. **Tolerances.** Each view-dependent tolerance goes through the given view's zoom, or 100 % without a view
   (`addressing::tolerance_du`):
   - the hit tolerance: `draw.selection.set {by: point}`, newly 3 view px, the spec's `draw.hitTest.get` default (it was
     0); and `draw.hitTest.get`'s own `tolerance`;
   - the Polyline snap radius;
   - the Snap to Contour radius (`draw.point.move`);
   - the gap tolerances, through the colour commands' `view` since C4.2a round 1.
   - Not view-dependent by the spec: the paint, unpaint and pick point targets, which take the object under the exact
     point (03 §8.1-§8.2 define no hit tolerance; their gap closing scales).
3. **Gestures that state the view whenever the command has such a tolerance:**
   - Polyline and Select, as above;
   - Paint, Unpaint, Paint Unpainted, Close Gap and Dropper, always.
   - The other gestures' commands have no view-dependent tolerance.
4. **Tests at zoom 4 and 1/4, gesture against an independently built command:**
   - `c42a_r2_audit_each_view_tolerance_at_zoom_4_and_a_quarter`: the hit tolerance (a click 2 px beside a thin line
     selects it at both zooms), the Polyline snap, the gap tolerance (a paint click closes a 1-px gap with the Small
     mode).
   - `c42a_r2_audit_the_contour_snap_radius_follows_the_named_view`: snaps at 100 %, not at zoom 4.
   - `c42a_r2_01_view_space_points_…`: view-space rectangle equals drawing-space; rotated rectangle refused; scene space
     refused; snap radius by view.
   - Inventory rows added for the three commands whose tolerance reads a named view.

**The capture bound.**
- At most two captures run in one executor (`inspect::MAX_CAPTURES`, one job thread each).
- A further capture waits for a slot on its caller's own thread, with no thread added, bounded by the call's budget and
  cancellation. At most eight callers wait (`host::MAX_WAITING_CAPTURES`); one more, or a wait reaching the budget,
  answers `limit.exceeded` with `details.limit: "concurrentCaptures"`.
- The handler also refuses while two capture jobs run, which covers a cancelled capture finishing in the background
  (ADR-0020 decision 8; note 5 §1c).
- **What bounds a user caller (the GUI):** the same gate. It applies to every caller, user or agent, before the request
  is queued. Captures are queries, not job commands, so they do not count against an agent session's `maxJobs`; the
  capture gate is their own bound.
- **Measurement:** the isolated test `c42_isolated::concurrent_captures_are_capped_and_their_threads_counted` races 14
  callers and counts OS threads: 10 answered, 4 refused; peak 18 threads over a base of 6 with 14 callers.
- The reviewer's six concurrent captures all complete (two run, four wait).
