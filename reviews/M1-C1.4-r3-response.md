### C14-R3-03: the phases, proved against the real binary

`bins/headless/tests/cancel_phases.rs` drives `toontown-headless --mcp-stdio` with elicitation. For each phase it
checks four things:
- the target keeps its bytes;
- the worker answers the next call;
- no response is sent for the cancelled request;
- the activity log has no successful export.

The phases:
1. **Before sending.** The call and its cancellation go in one write, 10 rounds. Either no `elicitation/create` is sent
   at all, or, when the call thread got there first, it is withdrawn with `notifications/cancelled`.
2. **While waiting.** The cancellation withdraws the ask.
3. **Cancellation, then a late accept** (the reviewer's order). The answer is discarded and the token is not consumed.
4. **Accept, then cancellation** (the answer has arrived but is not yet processed when the cancellation lands), raced
   20 times. Every round is consistent: either nothing was written and no response was sent, or the confirmed
   overwrite happened and was answered `ok`. Never a silent overwrite, never a response without the effect. A local
   run gave 19 dropped and 1 acted.
