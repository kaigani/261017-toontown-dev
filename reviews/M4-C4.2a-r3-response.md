## C42A-R3-01: `frame` and view space are honoured, not only declared

- **The fix.** Every point-taking command resolves its target as 02 §13.1 item 4 says: `drawingId`, else
  `elementId` + `frame` (the current element when `elementId` is absent), else the current drawing.
  - Eleven targets ignored `frame` and now pass it: `draw.selection.set`, `draw.pointSelection.set`, `draw.cut.apply`,
    `draw.erase.apply`, `draw.transform.apply`, `draw.envelope.apply`, `draw.point.move`, `colour.paintUnpainted.apply`,
    `colour.pick`, `colour.gap.close`, and the paint family's shared `Call` (`colour.paint.apply`,
    `colour.paint.remove`).
  - The shared precheck (`draw::precheck_steps`) now reads `elementId` and `frame` whenever the command declares them,
    so availability resolves the same drawing as the handler.
- **"Honoured" made mechanical.** `c42/addressing.rs::c42a_r3_01_frame_and_view_space_are_honoured_by_every_point_taking_command`
  covers the 27 commands of `scripts/check-addressing-params.py`. Each runs on two executors with the same setup:
  - the call under test: `frame` addressing the drawing exposed at another frame of Bob (the context stays at frame 1),
    and `space: "view"` points in a zoomed, panned view;
  - the reference: the same call with an explicit `drawingId` and drawing-space points from the same published matrix;
  - the check: equal answers and equal canonical saved bytes. Every answer that names a drawing names the one at that
    frame, and at least 20 of the 27 succeed (not only equal refusals).
  - The commands addressed by an object id (`draw.point.add`, `draw.point.set`, `draw.segment.move`) and the
    element-wide `draw.repositionAll.apply` take their drawing from the id or the element, so for them only view space
    is compared.

## C42A-R3-02: ruling D-16

- **The rule.** The outcome-dependent logic is gone. A gesture states `view` whenever its command has a view-dependent
  tolerance and the view's zoom is not exactly 100 %, and omits it at exactly 100 %. This applies to the Select click
  and the Polyline; the paint family keeps stating it always. (No contour-snapping gesture exists in C4.2a; its command,
  `draw.point.move`, honours `view` and has a test.)
- **Declared edit to a reviewer-owned test (authorised by D-16), the only one:**
  - File: `crates/executor/tests/c42a_r2_review.rs`, test `part_c_equivalence::c1_move`, line 843.
  - Before: `            json!({"by":"point","point":p,"mode":"replace"}),`
  - After: `            json!({"by":"point","point":p,"mode":"replace","view":"drawingView"}),`
  - No other round-1 or round-2 reviewer test expected a view-tolerance command without `view` at a zoom other than
    100 %; every reviewer test passes.

## The hit tolerance of `draw.selection.set {by: point}`

- **Spec lines:**
  - 02 §13.1 item 5 (lines 1151-1155) names "hit tolerance" first among the view-dependent tolerances that "use the
    given view's zoom, or 100 % zoom when no view is given";
  - its value is defined by the `draw.hitTest.get` row of 02 §13.2 (line 1313: `tolerance: number = 3 (view px)`), the
    only stated hit tolerance; the point selection uses that default (`draw_select::HIT_TOLERANCE_PX = 3.0`).
- **Behaviour change of an M2 command, declared in the handoff:** a point selection used to need an exact hit; it now
  takes the front-most object within 3 view pixels (3 du at 100 %).
- **What depended on exact-point selection: nothing found.**
  - The full local executor suite ran on the code with the new tolerance; it was not run again on the final head. Every
    binary passed except two, neither touching selection: the accepted CI skip `c33_r1_crash` and the watchdog timing
    test `c34_round3::a_watchdog_timeout_on_the_work_account_path_is_not_a_cancellation` (load-dependent).
  - The E1 scenario sends no `draw.selection.set`; its replay and byte checks passed on Windows and macOS in run
    43617667731 (`c33 render release`), as did E2 and E4.

## Capture bound: the three unverified points

- **A capture job that panics releases its slot.** The job runner now catches a panic for every job and ends it
  `failed` with a typed `internal` error; a running record would otherwise hold a slot for ever.
  `c42_isolated::a_panicking_capture_job_releases_its_slot`.
- **A waiting caller honours its own budget and cancellation and starts nothing afterwards.**
  `c42_isolated::a_waiting_capture_honours_its_cancellation_and_budget_and_starts_nothing_afterwards`:
  - cancelled after 50 ms, the waiter answers `command.timeout` in 51 ms, and the capture jobs stay at two;
  - with a 250 ms call budget, it answers `limit.exceeded` at 257 ms, and the job list does not grow.
- **Order:** not guaranteed. A freed slot goes to whichever waiter the condition variable wakes first; the screenshot
  note §1c says so.
