## A2 ? Mapping: changes required

### C42A-R1-01 ? P2 ? Published matrices lose precision under accepted pan

Clause: `reviews/M4-design-views.md` ?5 V1 (published matrices and conversions give the same points; inverse identity within float rounding); `spec/02_drawing.md` ?2.2 lines 102?105 (queryable affine mapping).

Reproduction / permanent reproduction: `mapping::independently_derived_mapping_grid`, `crates/executor/tests/c42a_r1_review.rs`. Fails at c745a55: inverse published matrix returns 1000000.000075668 instead of 1000000. Accepted states cover zoom .0625/1/64, rotation 0/90/37.5, centre 0 and (1e6,-1e6), viewport 1920?1080 and 811?423. Absolute comparison tolerance is 1e-5 pixels forward and 1e-5 du inverse. Expected points are independently derived: subtract centre, invert y, rotate clockwise, scale, translate by half viewport. This tolerance is deliberately much looser than direct f64 transform rounding at these coordinates.

Required versus observed: published mapping must agree with the conversion function and round-trip; subtraction of transformed basis points introduces translation-dependent coefficient errors. An agent using the published matrices obtains different drawing coordinates from `view.coordinates.convert`, exceeding the 1e-6 du gesture equivalence scale of 02 ?13.3. Fix direction: construct linear coefficients analytically and derive translation separately; do the same for inverse coefficients. Keep test body unchanged.

`mapping::rotated_colour_pick_and_hit_test_agree_with_independent_point` passes: rotated, zoomed and panned view pick equals drawing-space pick and the independently positioned view point hits content. Supplementary builder evidence `c42/views.rs::m3_item3_refusals_become_implementations_and_agree_with_the_mapping` covers activeView/draft/exclude and raster equality; independently locating a known rendered pixel is added with screenshot review.
