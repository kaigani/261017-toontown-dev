## Findings

### C24-R1-01 — P1: independent topology properties cover only four command routes

**Clause/ruling:** C2.3 round-3 readiness item 2; C2.4 plan §2; charter item 2; 02 §3.12 and §13. The required breadth is through every topology-changing operation, with independently predicted topology, boundaries/side sets and canonical zone identities.

**Reproduction:** `python tests/adversarial/c24-r1-audit.py ReadinessCoverage.test_every_topology_operation_has_a_seeded_independent_exact_oracle_route`. This intentionally failing inventory assertion identifies the two seeded consumers of the new exact oracle: vector `c24_properties.rs` and executor `c24_topology.rs`. The latter executes transforms, segment Cutter, disjoint Flatten and vectorize. Missing independent command routes include stroke add/set/delete, Eraser, Optimize, Smooth, Lines to Brush, Centre Line, Break Apart, Envelope, and point moves/snapping. [Receipt](evidence/M2-C2.4-r1/audit-validated.txt).

**Observed versus required:** `crates/vector/tests/oracle/mod.rs` independently constructs rational intersections, angular order and face areas without importing product geometry. That independence is useful, but its consumers primarily compare face counts/areas. They do not implement the plan's independently recomputed boundary-side sets and zone-ID hashing across all those operations. The retained `c23_r3_topology.rs::oracle` calls product `node`, `settle`, `build` and `zone_at`; it is a consistency check, not an independent expected-result oracle. The two 10k history oracles retain genuine independent coverage of their narrower stroke/square/cut histories and do not supply the missing operation breadth.

The Cutter property is weaker still: `after.len() >= before.len()`, conserved total area, and an upper bound on the sum of areas do not require a crossing cut to create the expected faces. An unchanged result can satisfy those checks. Similarly, area invariance alone cannot distinguish a correct reflection/translation from an identity operation.

**Fix direction:** add independent expected geometry/topology for the missing operation families and assert the actual expected partition, boundary membership, side fills and canonical IDs. Retain the existing consistency and history controls. Re-run new seeds at the required case count. Do not describe four routes plus product self-consistency checks as complete item-2 coverage. This finding concerns missing required verification; it does not assert that each unverified command is defective.

### C24-R1-03 — P2: the Flatten gate accepts malformed timing evidence

**Clause/ruling:** charter item 6, analyzer rejection of malformed input; ruling (a), the retained every-run C22-T2 gate must distinguish refusal from completion.

**Fix direction:** validate root/series/record shapes, sample types, finiteness, nonnegative duration, counts, OS/profile/run compatibility and individual completion/refusal records before calculating percentiles. Reject malformed input with exit 2 and no success receipt. Use one declared percentile rule; the gate currently takes the lower middle sample for even counts, while `analyse-measurements.py` uses the upper nearest rank.
