## 3. The casts and integer arithmetic read under E-31

The full table is the response §2.1. In short: every `as` cast and every integer `+`, `-`, `*` on a caller-derived value
in the six product files C5.1a added was read. Two sites were fixed (`frame_at`'s multiply and add, now saturating; the
cell drag's row arithmetic, now `try_from` and saturating), the rest are bounded by construction: frame deltas are
differences of two `u32` frames (within ±2^32), indices sit in branches that bound them, floats are finite-checked
before their saturating casts, and the parameters of `timeline.rs`, `xsheet_view.rs` and `transfer_options.rs` are
clamped or converted with `try_from` before any arithmetic.

## 6. `shownInTimeline`

On the list of spec A additions for the user (`plan/spec-issues.md`). **The window cannot do without it entirely, so it
is kept for your ruling** (response §1.3): the window must draw exactly the rows the interpreter hit-tests. The rows
under a collapsed parent it can derive from `depth` and `collapsed`, but the effect-parameter rows an effect's setting
hides depend on session state no query reads (`timeline.effectParameterRows.set` answers only what it was given). The
alternatives are listed there: leaving the hidden rows out of `timeline.rows.list`, or a read of the setting (also a
spec A addition). Until effect elements exist (M7) no such row exists.
