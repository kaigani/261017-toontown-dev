### C42B-R1-01 — P2: nonuniform scale changes the stroke outline

Clause: reviews/M4-design-render-transport.md §8 lines 98–105 requires the final renderer's stroke rules; ADR-0007 product amendment decision 7 requires pencil outlines from that walk. Reproduction: stroke (10,40)→(30,40), width 20, inside layer scale (3,0.5), translated (0,40). Required: affine-transformed round stroke. Observed: geometric-mean width yields the wrong thickness and caps, visibly disagreeing with exported artwork. Permanent reproduction: `cargo test -p tt-render --test c42b_r1_review anisotropic_layer_preserves_final_stroke_coverage --locked -- --nocapture`. Fails at a1500000: 0.0503125 fraction versus a generous 0.01 threshold. Fix direction: construct stroke pieces in local coordinates, then transform their outlines before tessellation; account for transform in flattening tolerance. Keep test body unchanged.

Builder's five render canvas tests pass, including committed golden corpus and V1–V10 corrupt files. Run on Windows locally; CI check matrix runs workspace tests on Windows and macOS. ADR-0006 decision 7 exempts interactive pixels from cross-machine determinism; ADR-0007 product amendment decision 6 separately promises identical tessellation bytes across platforms.

External specification obstacle to the requested decoder **from transport §2 and §8 alone**: §2 specifies a fixed 64-byte header with more fields than fit, without offsets or field widths for its counts; §8 does not replace this layout. ADR-0007 later says 80 bytes. Those two note sections alone do not define an implementable byte schema. A decoder derived from the product implementation would violate the requested independence. No decoder pass is claimed. Remaining corpus/command fixtures, five-drawing coverage comparison have not been completed; A1 is not an exhaustive acceptance review.
