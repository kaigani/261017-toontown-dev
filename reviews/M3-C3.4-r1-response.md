## 2. Orchestrator items

| Item | Disposition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **O-1** oracle for request cancellation during a batched wait | **Defect found and fixed.** An MCP client's `notifications/cancelled` never reached the executor: the server only withheld its answer. A sequential batch's waited job step (`wait: true`, from C3.4) therefore kept waiting on the document thread until the job ended or the watchdog budget passed. If the job ended first, the batch's later steps ran and committed after the cancellation. The reviewer's probe saw the later step not commit only because its 1000-frame job outlasted the budget, so the step timed out (`job.waitTimeout`) and the batch stopped on that error. A direct `run_command` with `wait: true` likewise blocked its caller until the job ended or the budget passed. **Fix:** the executor takes the caller's cancellation probe (`invoke_served_until`, `batch_until`, `batch_audited_until`), and the MCP server passes `is_cancelled(requestId)` for `run_command` and `batch`. The call polls the probe every 20 ms and marks its watchdog token as for a timed-out caller. Waits (direct, a batch's waited step, a batch's `job.wait` step) poll in 20 ms slices and answer `command.timeout` with `reason: "cancelled"` and the `jobId`. Later steps are refused at the existing acceptance boundary. Jobs are untouched. **Contract** (ADR-0020 decision 8): (a) the waited step answers at once and nothing after the cancellation changes the document; (b) the job stays the job system's, listed in `job.list` / `job_status`, terminal on its own or after `job.cancel`; (c) no `.tt-partial-*` file or sidecar once terminal; (d) a direct `wait: true` call behaves the same. | `crates/executor/tests/c34_cancel.rs` (4 tests): batch with `stopOnError` on and off (the waited step answers within 5 s of the cancellation, the later step is not run or is refused with `command.timeout`, the document is unchanged, the job is listed and completes on its own with 48 frames and the manifest and no staging); batch then `job.cancel` (job `cancelled` with `job.cancelled`, nothing published, no staging); direct `wait: true` (same answer, job completes, no staging); an uncancelled waited call still answers the manifest. `crates/mcp/tests/c34_cancel.rs`: the reviewer's scenario through MCP `notifications/cancelled` (no answer to the cancelled request; the later `element.create` never ran, `ref.notFound`; the job is `running` in `job_status`; `job.cancel` makes it `cancelled`; no staging and nothing published). |
