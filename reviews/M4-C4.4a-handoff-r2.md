## 8. Run 44592525640 (`53a1014e`): `check (macos-latest)`, ruled (D-64)

- **The test:** `crates/executor/tests/c34_r1_contract.rs::sequential_wait_timeout_retains_job_identity` (reviewer
  file, M3 C3.4 round 1, `1cbd1906`). The binary ran 2 tests in 0.90 s; this one failed at line 31,
  `Error: "step envelopes required"`. The same product passed it in run 44475666779.
- **A wall-clock race (D-45), not a product defect.** Line 22 sets the executor budget to 500 ms. A sequential batch step
  with `wait: true` waits until the call's start plus the budget less the margin (`run.rs` `finish_batched_job`, margin =
  budget / 10 = 50 ms; line 35 asserts `timeoutMs <= 450`), then builds the step's envelope and the batch's reply. The
  caller's watchdog (`ExecutorHandle::call_with`) answers `command.timeout`, an envelope without step envelopes, when the
  reply arrives after enqueue + 500 ms. So the test passes only if finishing the step and delivering the reply take under
  50 ms while the job's workers spin 20 ms per frame on every core (line 26); on the 3-core macOS runner they did not.
  The step's bound is computed from the call's own start (`call_start`, the enqueue time); with the product's budget
  (5 s) the margin is 500 ms.
- **Ruled (D-64) and applied** (`@@D64@@`): lines 22, 26, 28 and 35 only, as proposed: executor budget 5,000 ms
  (margin 500 ms), `timeoutMs <= 4500`, frames 1 to 240 with 100 ms spin per frame, the job cancelled after the check as
  now; the step-envelope and retained-identity assertions unchanged. Locally the binary's 2 tests pass in 4.67 s. The
  margin is retired from the M11 list (`reviews/M4-merge-note.md` §4).
