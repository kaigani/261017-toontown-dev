## 2. Behaviour notes a reviewer will want

- **Targets (04 §17.1 item 2).** Explicit `elementIds`/`elementId` + `from`/`to`/`frame` never touch the selection
  or the current frame; without them the shared cell selection (`xsheet` context, mirrored to `timeline`) is used,
  else the current element and frame; neither → `cell.noTarget`. Frame positions are 1 … 100 000 (`cell.frameOutOfRange`,
  10 §5's scene limit); counts and lengths keep 04's 1 … 9999 / 1 … 999 schema ranges (`arg.invalid`); a result longer
  than 100 000 frames is `doc.limitExceeded` before staging.
- **Undo.** Every action/toggle marked "yes" is one undo step (tested for 27 commands, undo/redo/undo each).
  `element.collapsed.set` is saved with the scene but is a system transaction without an undo step (04 §17.4 "no").
  `element.solo.set`, `element.typeColour.set`, selection and current-element commands never touch the document.
- **Names.** `Name`, `Name_1`, … skipping used names; sound elements `Sound-01`, `Sound-02`; clones always take a
  suffix; parent pegs `<element>-P`; parameter rows `<effect>-mask/-sprites/-bones/-skin`, particle sprite drawing
  element `<effect>-sprite`. Default drawing names follow 04 §14.2 (SI-091).
- **Creation.** Placement above the selected row at its level (top of the root level otherwise) and right of the
  selected column (rightmost otherwise); `parentId`/`above`/`xsheetAfter` override; with an explicit `sceneId` the
  selection is not consulted. The new elements become the selection and the first becomes current (04 §3.2).
  `element.hiddenInDrawingMode` is returned in the result's `warnings[]` and in the envelope.
- **Delete.** Children re-attach to the deleted element's parent at its position; effects take their parameter rows;
  parameter rows alone are refused; banks no remaining element uses are deleted with their drawings in the same
  transaction (10 §4.8); `x-ref: element` references from remaining elements are fixed as `edit.delete` does.
- **Copies.** `element.duplicate`, `element.drawing.duplicate`, `cell.drawing.duplicate`, cross-bank copies of
  `cell.range.move`/`cell.clipboard.apply` copy drawing JSON verbatim (drawing-local IDs, fills, owner records,
  `meta`/`ext`) and mark the copies verbatim for the settle pass. **Found during C3.1:** without that mark the settle
  pass re-noded copied drawings (the fixture's drawing rendered differently after duplication); the same mark was
  added to M2's `edit.paste` drawing copies (`edit_ops.rs::copy_drawing`, paste-as-new-element) — a declared product
  edit to M2 code, covered by `c31_obligations.rs::item6_…` and the existing `c12_edit_ops.rs` (16/16).
- **Exposure sheet.** `rev` is the highest revision of the scene and its cell-based elements; `expectRev` compares
  with it (`xsheet.revisionMismatch`). Validation precedes any change; errors carry `pointer`, `column` and `run`.
  Media columns are read-only for `.set` (SI-088).
- **needs_confirmation without a token.** `element.drawing.delete {ifInUse: "fail"}` on exposed drawings answers
  `status: "needs_confirmation"` with `code: "drawing.inUse"`, the exposures as `consequences` and
  `retryWith: {ifInUse: "clearCells"}` (04 §17.1 item 6). The executor maps errors carrying `x-needsConfirmation` to
  that envelope (`run.rs::needs_confirmation`), separate from the token protocol of 11 §6.3.

- **Timing budget, calibrated.** `TIMING_WORK_BUDGET` = 300 M units (≈ one cell/frame/node each). The calibration
  (`c31_budgets.rs`, 1001 columns × 100 000 frames, `measurements release`) **found a real defect in M1's model**:
  every staged commit diffed and range-patched exposures by expanding runs to one heap string per frame, several
  times, so whole-sheet edits took 54–64 s on the Windows runner (run 41610425235) — past the 30-s watchdog and
  never refused, because the work was outside the account. `crates/model/src/change.rs` now expands to interned
  indices (identical patches and results, four bytes per frame): the same calls take 0.51 / 0.57 s locally in
  release; an over-budget call (every column re-timed to 9999-frame exposures) is `limit.exceeded` in 2 ms. The raw
  observations of both OSes are in [evidence/M3-C3.1/](evidence/M3-C3.1/).
