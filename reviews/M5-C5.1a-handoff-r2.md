## 4a. Ruling E-29 (items 1, 2, 4 and the unit-tested part of 3)

Built as `reviews/M5-design-handler-panics.md` §4 states; response §2.6 lists each item with its tests.

- **Item 1:** handler panics contained at the invocation boundary (`run::run_step`); the call fails with `internal`
  (`handlerPanicked`), nothing changes, jobs it started are cancelled, the next call is served; the count on stderr, in
  the host-only `shell.handlerPanicked` notice and from `ExecutorHandle::caught_panics`; the status line, no dialog.
- **Item 2:** a panic outside an invocation degrades the engine; every later call answers `internal`
  (`engineStopped`) with the hint that names the way out.
- **Item 3 (unit-tested part):** the watchdog marks a stopped engine not responding at once. The live check (banner,
  Restart, Recover with a dev-only hook) is a C5.1b row and in its manual script.
- **Item 4:** the hint for headless and MCP clients; a new executor lists the last autosave after the document thread
  died.
- **ADR-0013** decision 3 amended; **merge note** §5 records the watchdog gap as an M4 defect found by M5; the spec A
  additions (`caughtPanics`, an `engine.degraded` event) are on the user's list.
