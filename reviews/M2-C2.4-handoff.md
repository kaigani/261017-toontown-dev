## 6. The C22-T2 gate (orchestrator ruling) — outcome of the bounded pass

This is the C22-T2 gate only: a 100k-stroke Flatten counts as completed when its release wall time (p50 of ≥ 5
repetitions) is within the same CI run's 100k `strokeAdd` p50 and under the 30 s watchdog, measured in the release
headless job on both OSes. **The interactive latency budget (R1-12) remains the separate M11 C11.1 scale gate.**

**The bounded pass.** (1) *Why settled layers were not fixpoints:* a noding pass's splits create piece ends at its
junctions, and an end resting on another stroke is itself a junction — shrunk seed: two lines crossing exactly at a
vertex of a third stroke that retraces both; the third was split there only by the next edit. Fixed: split-only passes
to a fixpoint, and a junction keeps the point every participant already shares (ADR-0017 history 9; seeded property
`a_settled_layer_is_a_fixpoint_of_noding`, 512 cases in `check`, 4096 locally, with strokes marked changed and
unchanged). (2) *Incremental map:* before building it, the merged layer's settling was broken into phases on the gate's
layout (`c24_flatten_phases.rs`, release, CI headless). (3) *Gate re-measured* with raw files on the new head.

Raw files: [evidence/M2-C2.4/c22-t2/run-41425059602-80714a2/](evidence/M2-C2.4/c22-t2/run-41425059602-80714a2/)
(gate, 5 budgeted + 5 unbudgeted Flatten runs, phase runs, this run's 100k series); the earlier run 41407040557 is kept
beside it.

| OS | 100k `strokeAdd` p50 | Flatten unbudgeted p50 (5 runs) | Gap | Whole planar settle of the merged layer | Noding an incremental map could skip | Product (budgeted) Flatten |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Windows | 21.08 s | 38.50 s (36.21–40.68; over the watchdog) | 17.42 s | 2.85–3.13 s | 0.27 s | refused `limit.exceeded`, atomic, 1.54–1.67 s |
| macOS | 9.39 s | 15.03 s (14.77–15.97) | 5.64 s | 0.95–1.35 s | 0.11 s | refused `limit.exceeded`, atomic, 0.57–0.67 s |

**Decision (per the ruling): stop.** The gate is missed on both OSes. Skipping already-noded pairs could save at most
0.27 s / 0.11 s, and the whole planar settle is 3 s / 1.3 s of the 38.5 s / 15.0 s, so the gap is in the executor's
per-edit costs around the map (staging, the integrity gate, history), the same costs that make one 100k `strokeAdd`
take 21 s / 9 s; an incremental map cannot close it. No incremental map is shipped (no equivalence proof is needed for
code that is not shipped). The product keeps the atomic refusal before the watchdog; refusal is not labelled completion.

**Proposed re-scope (declared deferral, for the orchestrator's ruling):** move C22-T2 from M2 C2.4 to **M11 C11.1** and
merge it into the R1-12 scale gate there, as "100k-stroke layer merge (Flatten) completes within the 100k `strokeAdd`
p50 and under the watchdog on both OSes". Rationale: the remaining cost is the executor-wide per-edit path that C11.1
already owns for the interactive budget; C2.4 keeps (a) the atomic, work-charged refusal before the watchdog, (b) the
fixpoint property and the phase breakdown as the starting evidence, (c) the gate script `scripts/c22-t2-gate.py` and
`c24_measure.rs` running on every CI run. Owner after the ruling: M11 C11.1; until then the ticket stays open, owner
M2 C2.4.
