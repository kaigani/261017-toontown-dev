## C2.2 readiness list


The round-3 list is unchanged. R3-01/02/03 are now closed as ruled; the following obligations remain for C2.2.

1. **SI-053 contours and bridges.** Build a larger corpus of holes, nested loops, disconnected pieces, zero-pressure gaps, coincident/retraced edges and opposite orientations. A zero-width bridge is two coincident directed edges: noding must not invent a painted strip, zone or gap. Check winding/area and point membership against independent swept-tip unions. Include accepted near-budget coils and refused larger coils; C2.2 owns the faster bridge splice and records any continued limit explicitly.
2. **Persisted spine and authority (SI-054).** Preserve each surviving unedited stroke's spine points, pressure knots (including equal-t steps), pen ID/tip/min/max and `ext[dev.toontown.brush].tipAngle`. Closed spines are stored open with a repeated first anchor; dabs retain their convention. If noding changes authoritative outline geometry, define/test spine invalidation rather than silently retaining a stale spine. Preserve opaque extensions. Generic outline+spine patches still reject with both pointers; outline-only edits discard spine and spine-only edits re-sweep.
3. **Work-budgeted geometry and zones.** Compute zones only from successfully staged outlines. A refused stroke must leave no new layer, partial graph, zone ID, paint, revision, undo record or drawing event; a following edit must execute. Re-noding, union/bridge work and every symmetry copy need bounded aggregate command work. Test large pressure maps, maximum hull vertices, long/closed spines and coils near the bridge refusal threshold. Keep named `limit.exceeded` diagnostics and worker responsiveness.
4. **Revision, IDs and drawing events.** A topology-changing edit, paint, generic patch, atomic batch, undo/redo and checkpoint restore must each obey the established single-commit/revision contract. `draw.drawing.changed` carries drawingId/rev/commandId and created/modified/removed IDs consistent with the final derived graph; explicitly define/test how derived zone changes are reported. Dry runs and failures emit no change event; sequential batches retain per-commit events. Replace `doc.derivedUnavailable` only when zones/sides are actually available.
5. **Zone and stroke identity.** Re-node only within a layer; groups preserve nested layer boundaries and stacking. Junctions share anchors. Unchanged strokes keep IDs; split/merged strokes receive new IDs; derived zone IDs follow boundary identity deterministically. ID edits with stale drawing revisions reject; stale zone IDs use `colour.staleZoneId`; point targets re-resolve. Test degenerate curves, tangencies, overlap, dabs and zero-width topology at stated numeric tolerances.
6. **Colour references and recovery.** Keep colour IDs, side fills and texture matrices rather than baked RGBA. Split zones inherit their old fill; merged zones take the larger part's fill with deterministic tie rules. All boundary sides of one zone agree, including load repair. Turn interim `colour.unresolved` into recovery/placeholder behavior per 03 §11.2; drawing recovery entries contain every stroke/side/text colour exactly once with all style values and texture references. Cover paste/import/load/save and undo as one transaction.
7. **Pencil, invisible, text and bitmap roles.** Pencil and invisible paths take full part in planar topology; pencil thickness is rendering data and invisible strokes remain absent from output. Text and embedded bitmap nodes remain transformable ordered nodes, not implicit planar edges unless an explicit conversion creates strokes. Keep rich-text NFC origin/format and paragraph invariants, missing-font names, image references/pixels and node/group/pivot identity through edits, content insertion and package round trips.
8. **Cross-OS determinism.** Compare canonical planar graph, ordered nodes/edges, side assignments, zone IDs/fills and area/membership on Windows x86_64 and macOS aarch64, with repeated runs and replay. Use ADR-0004 numerics; do not depend on hash iteration, timing or threads. Preserve the current C2.1 golden. Extend nonidentity mirror/kaleidoscope and same-font layout corpus under R1-11/C2.3 before M4 C4.2 enables symmetry.
9. **Scale and performance.** Recompute 100k-stroke time, allocation, retained/process memory and queue observations; never relabel object-count bounds as byte budgets. Keep both 10k geometry/history oracles and add topology/paint operations. Preserve the R1-12 C2.4 latency/allocation/queue budgets and M11 C11.1 final gate; derived-map cache/history snapshots must have explicit bounded retention.
10. **Replay with painting.** Exercise actual MCP and script sequences mixing stroke edits, gap closing, paint by ID/point, fill mapping, colour recovery, batching, dry run, checkpoint restore and undo/redo. Compare canonical content and derived graph with fresh-executor replay; preserve the shared reference/integrity gate and existing single-arbiter lock, staged-effect and SI-047 boundaries.
11. **Read-back and limits.** Full nested §13.4 content round-trips with reassigned local IDs and preserved references. Include computed zones/side zone IDs only when available; bounded payloads still return `limit.exceeded` with a usable hint. Keep schemas, generated IPC, retained examples, registry parity and the malformed-command watchdog sweep current.
