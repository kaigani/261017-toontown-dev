# C5.1b — The Timeline with a mouse and with a trackpad (≈ 10 min)

Spec: 04 §9-§11, lines 600-720 (the Timeline view); plan §10. Automated: the three-way rows
(`scripts/c51b-timing-three-way.py`, real input on Windows with `--external`) and the D-39 rows. This script checks
the feel a script cannot.

Open the sample project (the start screen's Sample project, or a project with a peg, two drawings and an effect). Do it once with
a mouse, once with a trackpad.

1. **Wheel** over the rows → the layer list and the tracks scroll together, row for row.
2. **Shift + wheel** (trackpad: a horizontal swipe) over the tracks → the frames scroll sideways; the ruler follows.
3. The **Zoom** slider, step by step → the cells widen and narrow; the frame under the playhead stays in view.
4. **Click** a frame in the ruler → the playhead jumps there; **drag** along the ruler → the playhead follows the
   pointer without lagging behind (frames crossed quickly may be skipped, never queued).
5. **Drag** a bar's body, its start and its end → a ghost follows the pointer; at the release the bar lands where the
   ghost was and Edit › Undo puts it back.
6. Drag a drawing's **cells** (select two, then drag them along) → they move; with Ctrl (macOS Option) they are copied.
7. Drag a **row** up between two rows, then onto the peg → it reorders, then becomes the peg's child (indented).
8. Drag a row twice in a row, the second press on the row's name → the second drag works as the first (a drag never
   selects the names' text).
9. Press **Esc** in the middle of any drag → the ghost disappears and nothing changes.
10. Right-click a row, a cell and the ruler → each opens its own menu; choose an item from each.
