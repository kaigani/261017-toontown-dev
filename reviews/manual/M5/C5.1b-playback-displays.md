# C5.1b — Playback on a 60 Hz display and on an uncapped one (≈ 6 min)

Spec: 09 §2 (playback), lines 104-130; plan §6.2 (every frame of the range presented, interval p95 ≤ 1.5 periods);
ruling D-33 (the control). Automated: the stepped clock (C5.1a), the real clock's states (`c51_gestures`).

1. Open the sample project; Play › Playback Rate › 24 fps; Force Frame Rate off; Loop on.
2. On a **60 Hz** display: Enter → the playhead runs smoothly through the range and loops; the frame counter in the
   title and the playhead agree; no frame of a slow drawing sequence is skipped (watch a drawing that changes every
   frame).
3. Play › Playback Range › Free, then drag the range's start and end markers in the ruler → playback stays inside the
   new range.
4. Force Frame Rate on, with a heavy scene (many effects visible) → the time is kept: frames may be skipped, the speed is
   right.
5. On a display at **120 Hz or more** (or with vertical sync off): repeat step 2 → the same speed (24 frames a second,
   not faster).
