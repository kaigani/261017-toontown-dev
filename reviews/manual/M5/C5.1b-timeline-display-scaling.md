# C5.1b — The Timeline at 125 % and 150 % display scaling (≈ 5 min)

Spec: 04 line 605 (fixed row height), line 627 (keyframe glyphs); plan §10. The kernel hit-tests in CSS pixels, so the
scale must not move a hit target.

Do steps 1-4 at 125 %, then again at 150 % (Windows: Settings › Display › Scale; restart Toontown after each change).

1. Open the sample project; the Timeline at the default zoom.
2. The rows, their disclosure and visibility controls, the names and the keyframe glyphs are crisp, not blurred, and
   line up with the tracks' rows.
3. Click exactly on a keyframe glyph and drag it → it moves (the press found the glyph, not the bar).
4. Grab a bar's end within its last few pixels and drag → the bar's end moves (not its body).
5. Resize the window and the Timeline's dock area → the tracks redraw at the new size at once and keep their size
   when you stop (no growing, no scroll bar appearing in the panel).
