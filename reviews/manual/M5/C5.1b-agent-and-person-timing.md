# C5.1b — An agent edits timing while a person drags a bar (≈ 5 min)

Spec: 11 §6 (agents and the person), P2 (the person's input first); ruling D-26 (E6's form), plan §10. Automated for the
views: `scripts/c43-e6-coexistence.py`.

Needs an MCP client attached (Preferences › Agent access on; the client connected to the endpoint the discovery file
names).

1. Open the sample project; show the Timeline.
2. Press on a bar's body and hold it (do not release).
3. From the client: `element.timing.set` on the same element (`startDelta: 5`) → the call waits (the person is acting);
   the bar's ghost still follows your pointer.
4. Release the bar a few frames to the right → your move commits first, then the agent's call answers and its change
   applies on top (the History panel shows your step, then the agent's, named "— agent: <name>").
5. Hold a press on a bar again for longer than the agent's wait (Preferences › Agent access: wait for the user) → the
   client gets `ui.busy`; nothing of the agent's call applies.
6. While nothing is pressed, the client runs `timeline.view.set {"firstFrame": 40}` → the Timeline scrolls there.
