# C4.2 — The canvas at several display scales and monitors (≈ 6 min)

Spec: 01 §2; 02 §4.2; plan §12 item 5.

**Prerequisites** (everything this script assumes; nothing else is needed):

- **A build of the handoff head:** the CI artifact `desktop-Windows` or `desktop-macOS` of the run named in `reviews/M4-C4.2b-handoff.md` §1 (Windows: `default-build/toontown.exe`; macOS: unpack `Toontown-macos-arm64.app.tar.gz`), or a local `pnpm --dir desktop tauri build --no-bundle`.
- **Displays:** one at 100 % scale (macOS: a non-Retina display or "More Space") and one at 150-200 % (macOS: Retina); two monitors of different scale for step 4.

1. Windows: Settings › Display › Scale at 100 %; macOS: a non-Retina display or "More Space".
2. Start Toontown, Drawing workspace, draw strokes and turn the grid on → lines are sharp (one device pixel for the
   1-pixel grid), strokes not blurry.
3. Repeat at 150 % and 200 % (macOS: a Retina display) → still sharp; the stroke under the pointer is where the pointer
   is (no offset when drawing).
4. With two monitors of different scale, drag the window from one to the other → the canvas re-renders sharp on the
   second one; drawing still lands under the pointer.
5. Float the Drawing view (double-click its tab) and move the float between monitors → the same.
