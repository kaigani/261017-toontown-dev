### C02-R2-02 — P1 — Claimed recomputation still trusts `report.achievedHz`; the original estimator cannot be reproduced exactly

**Locations:** `scripts/analyse-tablet-log.py:337`; `desktop/src-tauri/src/spikes/tablet/simulate_windows.rs:235`, `:263`, `:270`; handoff's injector-rate row. Requirement: binding recomputation-from-raw ruling, R1-01, and independently reproducible matrix numbers.

Changing only `runs[0].report.achievedHz` to **123456789** in an otherwise lossless fixture with injection times spaced exactly 4 ms makes the analyzer report **123456789 Hz**, rather than recomputing 250 Hz or rejecting the inconsistency. This field is an aggregate even though it is nested in the raw host report. The builder's claim that all numbers are recomputed is therefore false.

The host measures `frames / contact_start.elapsed()` using `Instant`, including the wait after the last contact injection. Neither elapsed duration nor its endpoints are logged. Installation and removal are posted to the main thread; the callback returns the original event. The token is retained in a thread-local and removed on stop. A helper flushes the mutex-protected buffer; drop/send counters and sequence numbers cross the channel. No callback retain cycle is apparent. Stop acknowledgement is not a completed-removal barrier: a rapid command-level stop/start could race the old cleanup against a new global token, and a main-thread startup timeout needs lifecycle handling before product reuse. The prescribed one-start run does not exercise that sequence; lifecycle behavior is not claimed verified by these tests.

Native proximity pen/eraser/cursor labels carry to later poses in the extended test. Identity is global, not keyed by device ID, and persists until the next native proximity event. Mouse events with proximity subtype are not processed into equivalent in/out/tool transitions. Apple's guide says proximity is normally native; concurrent devices or an atypical driver require further work, not a universal identity guarantee. [Apple tablet-event packaging](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/Cocoa/Conceptual/EventOverview/HandlingTabletEvents/HandlingTabletEvents.html).
