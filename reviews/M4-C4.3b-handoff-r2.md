## 6. Declared behaviour changes

1. `tool.symmetry.set` refuses without a project (`needs_project`, 01 §1.3).
2. A view window's type buttons show its type (they read `view.list`'s array as an object before).
3. The shortcut editor has Import (replace or merge, applied by OK) and Export (01 §8.3 item 8).
4. Drawing aids, symmetry, the tools palette and the view type buttons are disabled while the kernel refuses them;
   symmetry shows the kernel's setting.
5. The view chrome's Auto light table, Show strokes and Current drawing on top show their state and toggle.
6. The main toolbar: Grid, Onion skin and Auto light table send their next state and show it checked; Grid type,
   Overlay and Previous/Next drawings are built (a list, a toggle, two lists); Last frame names its owner (M5 C5.1,
   `timeline.currentFrame.move`); the camera list names M4 C4.4.
7. The status bar's jobs show every session's running jobs (`job.list {all: true}`, the GUI's listing, 11 §15.2).
8. Open Backup lists the backups with their save time and an Open each.
9. `canvas_load {part: selection|rest}`: a selected group or layer selects everything inside it.
10. The canvas: a commit's answer clears the overlay and preview only for its own gesture; adopting a generation and
    unmounting drop the preview and its parts at once.
11. The availability answer completes the toolbar's drawing-aid references with their next state and reports their
    check state; the store re-reads availability after aid, tool, selection and view events.
12. `inspect.ui.tree`: the window's tree ends with the menu bar (D-38); headless, a node saying there is none. The
    sensor report gains `menuBar`.
13. Every control of the D-39 table declares the parameters its click sends (`data-tt-params`; the main toolbar's the
    kernel-completed reference), so `inspect_ui` states what a click invokes.
14. The parity manifest is version 2 (`surfaces`, `windowGestures`, `callSites`, `uses`); GUI-only reasons gain
    `disclose` (ruled in D-35) and `answer` (a message box's buttons; SI-155 asks to confirm).
15. The Camera measurement fails an incomplete record and requires 30 samples on the workstation; the equivalence script
    fails a row that differs from the spec's statement even when the three paths agree.
16. CI: the measurement assessment's fixtures run in the `lint` job; the D-39 table's MCP form runs in `desktop gui` on
    both OSes; `c41 availability` asserts constancy against a small project (D-41) and O-23's 20 ms budget moves to the
    workstation receipt.
