## 2. What C4.2a delivers

After rounds 1 and 2 and the three closure checks, C4.2a delivers the headless kernel side of the interactive Drawing
view (ruling D-3).

- **Views and the one mapping:**
  - `view.*`: zoom, pan, rotate, flip, fit, reset and the mapping;
  - matrices built analytically (R1-01);
  - zoom steps: the 02 §4.3 ladder for the wheel, table steps for a Zoom click (D-15, SI-122);
  - the Drawing view's `activeView` render and view space.
- **Tools, pens and aids:**
  - `tool.*`: the active tool, groups, settings, pens, symmetry, and Draw Behind or on the Top Layer;
  - `drawAid.*`: grids, guides, rulers, the static light table, and onion skin;
  - typed results and spec defaults (R1-20);
  - restored members validated one by one (R1-04, R1-28);
  - events only on effective changes;
  - the current colour, `colour.current.set` (D-11).
- **Gesture to command, with exact per-tool equivalence (D-4):** every C4.2 tool's gesture produces the command a caller
  would send, with equal parameters, canonical bytes and history. This includes:
  - the Zoom marquee fit (R1-09);
  - constrained Polyline handles (R1-24);
  - gradient and texture Paint mapping (R1-25);
  - the Dropper's pick then select (R1-23);
  - views stated per D-16.
- **Headless `pointer`, `key` and `inspect.screenshot`:**
  - `pointer` requires the `input` scope (R1-06), returns complete envelopes (R1-22), and takes the real request
    schema (R1-21);
  - the capture runs off the document thread (R1-02);
  - at most two captures run and at most eight callers wait; beyond that, a typed `limit.exceeded`;
  - a panicking job ends `failed`;
  - a cancelled wait answers the cancellation even when its job ended first (round 5).
- **Availability:**
  - mid-gesture user priority (D-12): agent writes wait, then answer `ui.busy`; under a dialog, tool changes answer
    `ui.modalOpen`;
  - every precheck agrees with execution, including for a locked addressed drawing.
- **Common addressing** (02 §13.1 items 4 and 5; R2-01, R2-02, R3-01, R4-01, round 5):
  - all 27 point-taking `draw.*`/`colour.*` commands declare and honour `space`, `view` and `frame`;
  - the 26 that address one drawing also declare and honour `drawingId` and `elementId`, in the handler and in the
    precheck; `draw.repositionAll.apply` is element-wide;
  - view-pixel tolerances go through the view's zoom;
  - `scripts/check-addressing-params.py` runs in CI `lint`.
