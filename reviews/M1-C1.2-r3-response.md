There are 86 placeholders in 68 entries, down from 100 in 80. | `c12_r3_registry` (12); `declared.rs::owning_section_examples_validate`, `::placeholders_carry_their_spec_issue`, `::generated_schemas_reject_every_mutated_leaf_and_unknown_member`; `semantic-recheck.json` (14 of 14 accepted) |
| C12-R3-02 checkpoint stores old stamps | Fixed (P1) | `checkpoint.create` captures `Cx::stamped_document()`: the staged content stamped as the staged changes will commit it (`documentRev` and the touched objects' revisions), so the advertised revision is the content's revision. The cache keeps an id's first envelope and is never overwritten. | `overlapping_identical_waits_retain_one_original_envelope`; all round-1/2 request-id tests |
