**The R4-01 property test** runs 150 random rounds over strokes in three drawings and checks every diff against an
oracle of stroke and scene values:
- the rounds are atomic batches mixing checkpoints, edits and staged `doc.diff`, or single committed commands;
- each diff's base is a random checkpoint or committed revision;
- values come from {0, 1, 2}, so rewrites and reverts are frequent;
- a diff must report exactly the changed strokes, their drawings and the scene;
- every base is checked again at the end.
