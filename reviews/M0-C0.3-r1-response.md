## C03-R1-01 — analyzer accepts incomplete or contradictory logs — **fixed**

`scripts/analyse-canvas-log.py` now validates the whole log before printing, and exits 2 with **empty stdout** on any
failure:
- **Environment:** time origin, user agent, DPR, viewport, screen, `webgpuApi`, forced renderer (which must equal the
  launch mode's), the canvas launch mode (whose `auto` must agree with `source`), and host facts or a host error.
- **Parameters:** distinct known scenes, seed, flattening tolerance, positive counts and durations.
- **Matrix:** an `auto` log must contain exactly the expected back ends × declared scenes, in order. The expected
  back ends are the forced one, otherwise WebGPU when its API exists plus WebGL2. There must also be one pixel run per
  back end. An explicitly failed run (with its error) counts as an observation, a missing one does not. Interactive
  logs may hold subsets, and manual logs no runs.
- **Workload:**
  - transfers are numbered 0..n−1 with `frame` equal to the index;
  - every transfer has the scene's encoded size, taken from its logged header counts (new logs; the header must name
    the same scene, seed and frame) or from the seed-1 workload table (older logs), and all transfers of a scene are
    the same size;
  - decode statistics must equal the workload: paths, cubics and stroke/fill paths, plus segments, fill vertices and
    triangles at tolerance 0.5. Stroke + fill paths must add up.
- **Samples:**
  - durations must be non-negative, and upload completion may not precede its CPU part;
  - idle and pan/zoom timestamps must be monotonic, cover the declared durations, and the idle phase must end before
    pan/zoom starts;
  - every frame has a CPU time; every WebGPU frame has a GPU completion time, and no WebGL2 frame has one;
  - renderer facts must be present, and canvas geometry must agree with the DPR;
  - pixel runs need their sample count, in order, with the right byte size.

All seven reviewer negatives and the three controls pass. Every committed real log still validates, including the
reviewer's fresh and CI logs. The page now logs each transfer's certified header counts. Fresh local logs through
the strengthened pipeline: `reviews/evidence/M0-C0.3/canvas-r1-local-windows-rtx4090-{auto,webgl2}.json.gz`. All 9
runs met 60 fps.

## C03-R1-02 — non-finite points silently drop geometry — **fixed at both ends**

- **Rust:** `renderlist::encode` returns `Result<_, EncodeError>`. It refuses, with typed variants:
  - non-finite or out-of-bounds points;
  - non-finite group transforms (also after the per-frame rotation);
  - negative or non-finite widths;
  - non-finite or inverted bounds;
  - verb/point/group ranges outside the scene.

  The command maps this to `spike.encodeFailed`. Scene bounds are now the extent of all points (the header documents
  them as such); the committed `tiny.ttrl` is unchanged. New tests cover each variant and check that the generated
  scenes are valid.
- **TS:** the decoder checks **every point for finiteness and the header bounds before building any geometry**, and
  throws the same way as for bad indices. The envelope additionally requires ordered bounds. The reviewer's two tests
  (NaN at offset 204, +∞ at 212) and all seven corruption controls pass, plus my NaN/−∞/out-of-bounds cases.

## C03-R1-05 — u8 pipeline: alpha 250 over an opaque background — **evaluated as ruled**

- **Configurations:** added `f32_pipeline` and two `OptimizeQuality` configurations (scalar, detected) and ran the
  full corpus.
- **Opacity:** the f32 pipeline **preserves opacity**: alpha 255 in every opaque-background scene, as does `ttraster`.
  The u8 pipeline gives 254 (×4), 251 and 250.
- **Speed:** on the performance scene the f32 pipeline costs −1 % to +14 % relative to u8 on the CI runners
  (6.9 vs 6.5 ms scalar locally).
- **Cross-OS stability:** each f32 configuration is identical on all three OSes. Scalar f32 ≠ SIMD f32 on the same
  20 scenes as u8.
- **Reviewer test:** `c03_r1_05_source_over_an_opaque_background_stays_opaque` keeps its assertion. As the ruling
  says it "should pass against the quality pipeline", it now renders with `vello::Config::QualityFallback`, and
  passes.
- **Limitation pinned:** `c03_fixed_configurations.rs::u8_pipeline_loses_opacity_…_known_limitation` asserts u8 = 250
  and f32 = 255, so an upstream change is noticed.
- **Upstream:** no issue reports this opacity loss. The closest is linebender/vello#904 (closed), which adopted a ±1
  approximate division by 255 in the u8 path; that is a plausible cause, not established. None was filed.
- **Comment fixed:** the false identity comment at `main.rs:61` is corrected (unpremultiplication is not an identity).
