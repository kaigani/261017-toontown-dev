## Remaining ruled finding

**C14-R4-01 / C14-R3-02 — P1, builder C1.4, before M1 merge.** ADR-0015 §13 explicitly gives group params `{label, steps, expectRev}` and says they include expectRev semantics; SI-051 records the same ruling. `group_step` deserializes params into `BatchRequest`, which has `deny_unknown_fields` and no expectRev member. Script loading accepts the generic params, then execution returns `batch.stepFailed` wrapping `arg.invalid: unknown field expectRev`. A matching current revision cannot execute; a mismatching revision never reaches revision-conflict checking. The document remains unchanged, but that safe refusal is not the ruled contract.

Reproduce: `cargo test -p tt-mcp --test c14_r4_closure --locked`. Two tests pass and two fail: matching expectRev must run; mismatching expectRev must refuse the entire group with `rev.conflict`. See [closure-final.log](evidence/M1-C1.4-r4/closure-final.log). Required closure is support and enforcement at the group transaction boundary, retaining the passing replay, nested-group, atomicity and history tests. Do not remove expectRev from the written ruling to fit the parser.
